Companies and contacts are the foundation of your CRM. Every deal, activity, email, and call connects back to a company or contact record.

## Companies

### Adding a company

Go to **Companies** and click **New Company**. Only the company name is required.

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Company Name** | The company's name (required) |
| **Domain** | Their website domain, such as `acme.com` |
| **Industry** | Chosen from a list |
| **Company Size** | Chosen from a list of size bands |
| **Location** | Where they're based |
| **Website** | Their full website address |
| **Relationship Type** | Customer, vendor, partner, supplier, or competitor (see below) |
| **LinkedIn URL** | Their LinkedIn company page |
| **Primary Email** and **Primary Phone** | The main ways to reach the company |
| **Founded Year** | When the company was founded |
| **Employee Count Range** | For example, 51–200 |
| **Owner** | The team member responsible for the company |
| **Description** | Any notes or context |

Any [custom fields](/getting-started/account-setup#tags-and-custom-fields) you've set up for companies appear at the bottom of the form.

When you create a company, Alva checks for possible duplicates in your CRM and in your Discovery results. If it finds any, it shows them so you can open the existing record or click **Create Anyway**.

<Tip>
  To add many companies at once, click **Import** on the Companies page and upload a CSV file. See [Importing data](/crm/importing-data).
</Tip>

### Company detail page

Click any company to open its page. Depending on what Alva knows about the company, you'll see:

- **The header** — industry, size, and other key facts, the company's lifecycle stage, its [tags](/crm/tags), and its enrichment state (**Enrichment queued**, **Enriching…**, **Enriched**, **Complete**, **Enrichment failed**, or **No website**). If enrichment failed, you can retry it
- **Company Details** — website, location, founding year, LinkedIn, domain, size, and industry, plus the company's **Owner** and its email addresses and phone numbers. You can add more emails and phone numbers, remove them, and choose which is primary
- **About** — a description from the company's website and LinkedIn
- **Match details** — if the company came from [Discovery](/discovery/discovery-companies), the criteria it matched and the ones it missed
- **Signals** (closed beta) — events [Signals](/discovery/signals) found for this company, what they were found through, and the checks they passed
- **Discovery activity** — BDR outreach and agent actions for this company
- **Deals** — every deal with the company, with a button to start a new one
- **Contacts** — everyone linked to the company
- **Emails** — your email conversations with the company, if your email is connected
- **Activities** — notes, calls, meetings, and emails, with a form to log a new one
- **Calls** and **Messages** — call history and text message conversations
- **Custom Fields** — the values of your custom fields
- **Commitments** — promises and actions linked to the company. See [Focus](/getting-started/focus)
- **Relationship cadence** — how often you want to be in touch (see below)

Click **Edit** to change the company's details.

### Searching

The Companies search bar matches **name**, **domain**, or **industry**. Type and press Enter to filter the list.

If you're using the [module switcher](/crm/modules-and-relationships), the list shows only companies with the active module's relationship type — for example, switching to VRM shows only vendors.

### Relationship types

Every company can have a relationship type that describes how it relates to your business:

| Type | What it means |
|------|--------------|
| **Customer** | A company that buys from you |
| **Vendor** | A company that sells or distributes your products |
| **Partner** | A company you partner with |
| **Supplier** | A company that supplies you |
| **Competitor** | A direct competitor |

Companies imported from [Discovery](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile) get their relationship type automatically. You can also set it on the company form or map it from a column when you [import a CSV](/crm/importing-data).

### Lifecycle stages

Each relationship type has its own lifecycle stages:

| Relationship | Stages |
|-------------|--------|
| Customer | Target → Prospect → Lead → Customer |
| Vendor | Target → Evaluating → Negotiating → Vendor |
| Partner | Target → Engaging → Negotiating → Partner |
| Supplier | Target → Evaluating → Negotiating → Supplier |

Change a company's stage from the selector on its detail page.

## Contacts

### Adding a contact

Go to **Contacts** and click **New Contact**, or click **Add Contact** on a company's page.

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **First Name** and **Last Name** | The person's name |
| **Email** and **Phone** | How to reach them |
| **Job Title** | Their role |
| **Company** | The company they work for |
| **LinkedIn URL** and **Twitter Handle** | Their social profiles |
| **Notes** | Anything else worth knowing |

Custom fields you've set up for contacts appear at the bottom of the form.

### Contact detail page

Click any contact to see their full profile:

- **Contact Information** — name, role, email, phone, social links, and their company
- **Notes**
- **Custom Fields**
- **Commitments** and **Relationship cadence**
- **Emails** — every email conversation with this person
- **Calls** and **Messages**
- **Activities** — with a form to log a new one

### Revealing contact details (Pro)

When Alva knows a contact has an email address or mobile number available that isn't on the record yet, the contact's page shows **Reveal details**. Click it to look up their details. Revealing contact details uses credits and needs the Pro plan. See [Plans and pricing](/billing/plans-and-pricing).

### Searching and filtering contacts

The Contacts search bar matches name, email, phone, company name, and the values of your custom fields — on the contact or on their company.

You can also filter by one custom field. Pick a field and a value — for example, the SDR stored on the company — to see only the matching contacts. Text fields match anything containing your value; dropdowns, numbers, dates, and yes/no fields must match exactly. A company field matches every contact at that company.

You can ask Alva to do the same: "Show me the contacts whose SDR is Priya."

## Activities

Activities are the history of your interactions with a company, contact, or deal.

### Logging an activity

At the top of every **Activities** card, click **Log Activity**, choose **Note**, **Call**, or **Meeting**, and add:

- A **subject** (optional)
- A **description** of what happened (required)
- The **date and time** — this defaults to now, but you can backdate it

### Emails are logged for you

You don't need to log emails by hand. Emails you send from Alva, emails your BDR agents send, and emails picked up from your connected inbox are logged as activities automatically.

## Relationship cadence

A cadence is a reminder to stay in touch. Set one on any company or contact — **Weekly**, **Fortnightly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or a **Custom** number of days. When a relationship goes quiet for longer than its cadence, Alva raises a nudge on your [Focus](/getting-started/focus) page.

---

## Deleting records

You can delete a company or contact from its detail page.

<Warning>
  Deletion is permanent. Deleting a company also deletes its deals, contracts, and activities. Its contacts are kept but are no longer linked to a company. Make sure you no longer need the records before you delete.
</Warning>
