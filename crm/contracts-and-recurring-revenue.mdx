---
title: "Contracts and recurring revenue"
description: "Track contracts, renewal dates, and recurring revenue in Alva."
---

Contracts track your ongoing customer relationships once a deal is won. They're where you manage ARR, MRR, renewals, and changes in revenue over time.

## Creating a contract

### From a deal

Any deal that doesn't have a contract yet shows a **Create Contract** button. The contract is filled in with the deal's company and primary contact, and linked to the deal. Its status is **Active** if the deal is **Closed Won**, and **Pending** otherwise.

### On its own

Go to **Contracts** in the sidebar (under CRM) and click **New Contract**. You can still link it to a deal with **Link to Deal (Optional)**. A contract created this way starts as **Active**.

### With Alva

Ask Alva: "Create a contract for Acme — £24k a year, billed quarterly, starting 1 December." Alva can link it to the deal it came from and a primary contact.

## Contract fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|------------|
| **Name** | A descriptive name for the contract |
| **Company** | The company the contract is with |
| **Primary Contact** | The main contact for this contract |
| **Link to Deal** | The deal the contract came from (optional) |
| **Service Level** | The tier or level of service |
| **ARR** | Annual recurring revenue |
| **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** | Worked out automatically from the ARR |
| **Billing Frequency** | Monthly, quarterly, annual, bi-annual, or custom |
| **Contract Start Date** | When the contract begins |
| **Contract End Date** | When the contract ends, if it has an end date |
| **Auto-renew** | Whether the contract renews automatically |
| **Notes** | Any extra context |

The person who creates the contract becomes its owner. You can change a contract's **Status** when you edit it.

## Contract statuses

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Active** | The contract is live and earning revenue |
| **Pending** | The contract is agreed but not yet live — for example, its deal hasn't closed yet |
| **Suspended** | The contract is temporarily on hold |
| **Cancelled** | The customer cancelled |
| **Expired** | The contract has ended |

## Revenue tracking

### ARR and MRR

When you set the **ARR**, the **MRR** is worked out automatically by dividing by 12.

### Expansion and contraction

Each contract keeps its original ARR alongside any **expansion** (the customer spends more — an upsell, more seats, a higher tier) and **contraction** (the customer spends less). These appear on the contract's page, so you can see net revenue movement over time.

To change a contract's value, ask Alva — for example, "Acme have added 10 seats, take their contract to £30k." Alva updates the ARR and records the difference as expansion or contraction automatically.

### Cancelling

Ask Alva to cancel a contract and Alva will ask for the reason and confirm before it does it. A cancelled contract's ARR is recorded as churn. You can also change the status to **Cancelled** when you edit the contract.

## Renewals

The **next renewal date** is set to the contract end date. If **Auto-renew** is on, the contract continues automatically. If not, keep an eye on the renewal date and start a new deal for the renewal conversation.

## Searching and filtering

The Contracts page lets you:

- **Search** contracts by name or company
- **Filter by status** — show only active, pending, cancelled, or expired contracts

## Linking deals and contracts

When a contract is created from a deal, or linked to one, the two are connected:

- The deal page shows the linked contract, with its status, ARR, MRR, billing frequency, and renewal date
- The contract page lists its related deals
