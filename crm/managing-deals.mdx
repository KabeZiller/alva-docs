title: "Managing deals"

Deals are the opportunities you're working on. Every deal moves through a pipeline of stages, and Alva keeps watch over each one — scoring its health, spotting risks, and telling you what needs attention.

## Creating a deal

Go to **Deals** and click **New Deal**. You can also start a deal from a company's page.

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Deal Name** | A short description, such as "Acme — Enterprise Platform" (required) |
| **Company** | The company the deal is with (required) |
| **Primary Contact** | Your main contact for the deal |
| **Deal Owner** | The team member responsible (required) |
| **Stage** | Where the deal is now. New deals start at **Discovery** |
| **Sales Methodology** | MEDDPICC, BANT, or SPICED |
| **Deal Value** | The deal amount |
| **Probability (%)** | Your estimated chance of winning |
| **Expected Close Date** | When you expect it to close |
| **Notes** | Any extra context |
| **Products & Services** | Which of your [products](/context/products-and-competitors) the deal involves |
| **Competitors** | Who else the prospect is considering |

## Pipeline stages

Every deal moves through six stages:

| Stage | What it means |
|-------|--------------|
| **Discovery** | You're learning about the prospect's needs |
| **Qualification** | You're working out whether this is a real opportunity |
| **Proposal** | You've presented a solution and pricing |
| **Negotiation** | You're working through terms |
| **Closed Won** | You won the deal |
| **Closed Lost** | The deal is over and you lost it |

### The board and the list

The **Deals** page shows your pipeline as a board — each stage is a column, and each deal is a card you can drag to the next stage. Use the toggle at the top to switch to a list instead, and the search bar to find a deal.

Each card shows the deal name and company, its value, its expected close date, and its **health score**.

## Deal detail page

Click any deal to open its page.

### Overview

At the top you'll see the deal's name, company, contact, owner, value, stage, probability, and close date. Click **Edit** to change any of them, including the linked products and competitors.

### Health score

Alva scores every deal from **0 to 100**, based on:

- How complete your methodology fields are
- Email engagement and sentiment
- How long the deal has been in its current stage
- Whether there's recent activity
- Risk signals from email analysis

The score appears as a coloured badge:

- **Green (70–100)** — healthy and on track
- **Amber (40–69)** — needs attention
- **Red (0–39)** — at risk

Scores update automatically in the background. Keep the deal up to date and Alva takes care of the rest.

### Methodology tracker

The methodology tracker shows each criterion for the deal's methodology as a field you can fill in, with a completion percentage. See [Sales methodologies](/crm/sales-methodologies).

<Tip>
  You don't have to fill the fields in yourself. If your email is connected, Alva suggests evidence from your conversations — look for the suggestion badge. Or just tell Alva what you learned on a call and it will fill in the right fields.
</Tip>

### Email intelligence

If you've [connected your email](/email/connecting-an-email-account), the deal page shows every email conversation related to the deal, and a summary of buying signals, risk signals, commitments, unanswered questions, and sentiment across them.

### Activities and commitments

The deal's **Activities** card lists every note, call, meeting, and email, with a form to log a new one. **Commitments** tracks promises and actions on the deal, which also appear on your [Focus](/getting-started/focus) page.

### Deal briefings

Click **Generate Briefing** to have Alva write a PDF briefing on the deal — useful before a call or meeting. It covers the deal's context, its health score and risk level, methodology status, email intelligence, and recommended next steps. Earlier briefings are listed on the deal page so you can download them again.

## Risk alerts

Alva checks your deals in the background and sends you a notification when one needs attention — for example, when a deal goes dark, its health score becomes critical, its timeline slips, engagement drops, or key methodology fields are still missing. Open notifications from the bell at the top of any page. The deal's health score reflects the risk too.

## Creating a contract

Any deal that doesn't have a contract yet shows a **Create Contract** button. The new contract is filled in with the deal's company and primary contact, and linked to the deal. It starts as **Active** if the deal is **Closed Won**, and **Pending** otherwise. Once linked, the deal page shows the contract's status, ARR, MRR, billing frequency, and renewal date. See [Contracts and recurring revenue](/crm/contracts-and-recurring-revenue).
