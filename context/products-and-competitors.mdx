Products and competitors give Alva the detail it needs to write better outreach, analyse deals more accurately, and help you win against the alternatives.

## Products

### Adding a product

Go to **Context → Products** and click **Add Product**. You don't need every SKU — think in product groups or service lines, typically between 5 and 20.

**Basic information**

- **Product Name** and **Description** — what it is and what problem it solves
- **Category** — Software, Service, Hardware, Consulting, or Other
- **Pricing Model** — Subscription, One-time, Usage-based, or Custom

**Positioning**

- **Value Proposition** — why customers buy it
- **Target Use Cases** — the situations where it's most valuable
- **Target Industries** — the sectors it suits best

**Commercial details**

- **Typical Deal Size (min)** and **Typical Deal Size (max)**
- **Sales Cycle (days)** — how long it usually takes from lead to close
- **Starting Price** and **Pricing Notes**

<Tip>
  In each description, mention two or three specific examples of what's included. That gives Alva concrete talking points for outreach.
</Tip>

### How Alva uses products

- **Deals** — link products to a deal to track what you're selling. See [Managing deals](/crm/managing-deals)
- **Outreach** — BDR agents reference your products when they personalise emails
- **Email analysis** — Alva recognises when prospects discuss your products
- **Conversations** — Alva can answer questions about your products and suggest the right one for a prospect

### Managing products

From the Products page you can edit or delete any product. If you want Alva and your agents to stop mentioning a product without losing its details, ask Alva to archive it instead: "Archive our legacy reporting product." You can also ask Alva to add or update products for you.

## Competitors

### Analyse with Alva

The quickest way to add a competitor is to click **Analyse with Alva** on the Competitors page. Alva opens and asks which competitor to start with. Give Alva the name or website and it researches them, compares them with your profile and products, and saves a full battle card for you to review. A competitor in the list that doesn't have a battle card yet has its own **Analyse with Alva** button.

You can also ask Alva directly: "Analyse Acme at acme.com and build a battle card."

### Adding a competitor by hand

Go to **Context → Competitors** and click **Add manually**. The form has four parts:

**Basic Information**

- **Competitor Name**, **Website**, and **Description**
- **Market Position** — Market Leader, Challenger, Niche Player, or Startup
- **Pricing vs Us** — More expensive than us, Similar pricing, or Less expensive than us

**Competitive Analysis**

- **Their Strengths** and **Their Weaknesses**
- **Our Differentiators** — how you win against them
- **Typical Customer Objections** — what prospects say when this competitor is in the deal

**Win / Loss Patterns**

- **Common Win Reasons** and **Common Loss Reasons**

**Battle Card Summary**

- **Battle Card** — the full competitive analysis. Alva usually writes it, and you can edit it here

If Alva created the competitor, the form shows exactly what Alva saved, so you can correct or add to it.

### How Alva uses competitors

- **Deals** — link competitors to a deal to track who you're up against
- **Email analysis** — Alva spots competitor mentions in your conversations
- **Conversations** — ask "How do we compare to Acme?" and Alva answers from your battle card
- **Outreach** — agents can use your positioning against the alternatives

### Competitors from Discovery

If you create a [Competitor Profile (CP)](/discovery/profile-types-explained) in Discovery, the companies it finds are added to your competitors list automatically.

## Linking products and competitors to deals

When you create or edit a deal, tick the **Products & Services** the prospect is interested in and the **Competitors** they're considering. This feeds Alva's deal analysis and helps Alva see patterns over time — which products win most often, and which competitors you tend to beat.
