Strategy documents tell Alva where your business is heading — your go-to-market approach, growth priorities, positioning, and goals. The **master document** is included in every conversation with Alva, which makes it the most influential piece of context you can give her.

## Viewing your strategy documents

Go to **Context → Strategy** to see your strategy documents, with the master marked.

Open a document to read it, or click **Download PDF** to get a branded PDF copy.

## Where your first strategy comes from

You may already have one:

- **If you started on alvahq.com**, the brief from your conversation with Alva — your ideal customers, where to look, who signs off, your plans, and why customers pick you — becomes your master strategy document. See [Your website demo](/getting-started/website-demo).
- **If you received a first-look report from Alva** before signing up, that report becomes your strategy document.
- **If you brief Alva by email**, she uses your answers to her first questions to write your strategy. See [Alva by Email](/ai-assistant/alva-by-email).

## Creating a strategy document

Click **New Document**, or ask Alva to help:

- "Based on what you know about us, draft our business strategy document"
- "Write a competitive positioning document based on our pipeline"

Each document has:

- **Title** — what the document covers
- **Content** — written in Markdown, with an **Edit** tab and a **Preview** tab
- **Master Document** — a switch that makes it the master

Your first document starts from a template with sections for your current focus, target markets, key strategies, things to remember, and notes and decisions. Further documents start blank.

### The master document

One strategy document is the **master**. Alva reads it in every conversation, and your BDR agents use it for company context. Make it your most complete strategic overview — your market position, growth priorities, target segments, and key objectives.

Other documents aren't read automatically. To bring one into a conversation, mention it with **@** — for example, "Looking at @Q1 Sales Playbook, how should we approach this prospect?"

To change the master, click the star next to another document in the list (**Set as master document**), switch on **Master Document** when you edit it, or ask Alva: "Make the Q1 plan our master strategy." The previous master stays as a supporting document.

## How Alva uses strategy

| Feature | How it uses strategy |
|---------|---------------------|
| **Conversations** | Alva connects tactical questions to your strategic goals |
| **Growth ideas** | For senior leaders, Alva suggests growth ideas informed by your strategy. Accounts that get Alva's [weekly business development report](/ai-assistant/agenda-and-weekly-report) receive these as numbered recommendations in the report instead |
| **Deal analysis** | Alva frames deal insights against your priorities |
| **Discovery** | Your strategy helps Alva judge which companies matter most |
| **Outreach** | Your BDR agents use it for company context |

## Suggested updates

As your pipeline changes, Alva may suggest updates to a strategy document for you to review — for example:

- "Healthcare now makes up 40% of your deals. Consider updating your target market section."
- "Three deals were lost to Acme this quarter. You may want to update your competitive positioning."

You can also ask her to update a document directly.

## Who can edit

Admins can always create, edit, and delete strategy documents. An admin can give any other team member the same rights with **Allow Strategy Edit** in **Settings → Team**. See [Account setup](/getting-started/account-setup#managing-your-team). Everyone else on the team can read the documents but not change them.

## Deleting a document

Click the delete button next to a document in the list and confirm. You can also ask Alva to delete one — if it's the master, she warns you first and suggests which document to promote, so Alva and your agents don't lose their context.

<Tip>
  Keep your master document concise and current. Alva reads it in every conversation, so out-of-date strategy leads to out-of-date advice. Aim for 500–1,500 words of clear, actionable direction.
</Tip>
