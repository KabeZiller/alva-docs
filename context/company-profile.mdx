Your company profile is the foundation of Alva's understanding of your business. It's included in every conversation with Alva, every email analysis, and every BDR agent decision — so the more complete it is, the better Alva performs.

## Filled in for you at sign-up

When you create your account, Alva reads your company website and fills in a first version of your profile — a description, your products, and your positioning. She also saves your **Website**, finds your logo for **Logo URL**, and saves your **Privacy policy URL** if your site links to one. If you started on [alvahq.com](/getting-started/website-demo), your answers in that conversation fill in the gaps.

You can refine any of it at any time.

## Editing your profile

Go to **Context → Company Profile**. Anyone on your team can edit it.

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Company Name** | Your trading name (required) |
| **Tagline** | A one-line summary of what you do |
| **Website** | Used on email signatures and to find your logo |
| **Logo URL** | A public image address for your logo, used in HTML email signatures |
| **Privacy policy URL** | Your own privacy policy page. LinkedIn Lead Gen forms require it — see [Creating a campaign](/advertising/creating-a-campaign) |
| **Elevator Pitch** | What your company does and who it's for, in 50–100 words |
| **Value Proposition** | The core value you deliver |
| **Target Industries** | The sectors you sell into |
| **Target Company Sizes** | The size of company you sell to |
| **What makes you different/better?** | Your competitive advantages |
| **Key Features** | Your standout capabilities |
| **Customer Count Range** | Roughly how many customers you have — useful proof in outreach |
| **Notable Customers** | Recognisable customers Alva can use as proof points |

<Tip>
  Write your elevator pitch the way you'd explain your business to a potential customer in 30 seconds. Alva uses it constantly — to personalise outreach and to understand the context of your questions.
</Tip>

## Buyer personas

The roles you sell to — such as "Head of Sales" or "Operations Director" — aren't on the form. Tell Alva instead: "Our buyers are usually the Head of Operations and the Finance Director." She adds them to your profile and uses them when she plans searches and writes outreach.

## Updating your profile with Alva

You can ask Alva to change any part of your profile: "Update our elevator pitch to mention our new enterprise tier" or "Add Acme and Globex to our notable customers." She never invents a privacy policy address — if it's missing when she needs it, she asks you.

## How Alva uses your profile

| Feature | How it uses the profile |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Conversations** | Alva understands your business and gives relevant advice |
| **Email analysis** | Picks out buying signals that matter for what you offer |
| **BDR agents** | Personalise outreach with your positioning and proof points |
| **Email signatures** | Your name, tagline, website, and logo go into the signatures Alva designs |
| **LinkedIn ads** | Ad copy draws on your facts and proof points; Lead Gen forms link to your privacy policy |
| **Discovery** | Helps Alva judge how well companies fit what you sell |
| **Knowledge base** | Helps Alva pull product-specific insights from your emails |

## Keeping it up to date

Update your profile as your business changes — a new product line, a shift in target market, a change in positioning. Alva uses the new version straight away.
