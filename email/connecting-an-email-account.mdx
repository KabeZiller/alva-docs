Connect your email so Alva can read your business conversations, keep your CRM up to date, and send email for you and your [BDR agents](/bdr-agents/overview). Everything starts in **Settings → Email Connections**.

## Ways to connect

The **Connect Email Account** card offers these options:

| Button | Use it for | How it works |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| **Connect Gmail** | Gmail and Google Workspace accounts | Sign in with Google and approve access. Alva never sees your password. |
| **Connect Outlook** | Microsoft 365 and Outlook accounts, where the button is shown | Sign in with Microsoft and approve access. |
| **Other (IMAP/SMTP)** | Any other provider, or a Google account using an app password | Enter your mail server details and password in a short form. |

If a button appears greyed out as **Not Configured**, that sign-in option isn't available on your workspace yet — use **Other (IMAP/SMTP)** instead.

<Tip>
  Need dedicated mailboxes for cold outreach, separate from your main domain? Alva can buy and set them up for you. See [Outreach mailboxes](/email/outreach-mailboxes).
</Tip>

### Gmail or Outlook

1. Click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Outlook**
2. Sign in with your email provider
3. Allow Alva to read and send email
4. You come back to Alva with the account listed under **Connected Accounts**

### Other providers (IMAP/SMTP)

1. Click **Other (IMAP/SMTP)**
2. Pick your provider from the **Email Provider** list — common providers fill in the server settings for you. Choose **Custom / Other** to enter them yourself
3. Enter your **Email Address**, an optional **Display Name**, and your password
4. Click **Test Connection**, then **Connect Account**

Many providers, including Gmail, ask for an **app password** here rather than your normal password. The form explains how to create one when your provider needs it.

<Note>
  IMAP and SMTP are simply the standard settings mail apps use to receive and send email. If you're not sure of yours, your email provider or IT team can tell you.
</Note>

## Mailbox settings

Each connected account has its own row of settings under **Connected Accounts**:

- **Sender Name** — the name recipients see in the "From" line. It also fills the `{{sender.name}}` merge field in [signatures](/email/email-signatures) and [templates](/email/email-templates).
- **Daily Send Limit** — the most emails BDR agents can send from this mailbox each day (up to 500). Outreach mailboxes have lower caps while they warm up — see [Sending health and limits](/email/sending-health-and-limits).
- **Signature** — which signature this mailbox attaches, and whether it uses the HTML or plain-text version.
- **Sending health** — a **Ready**, **Caution** or **Do not send** badge for outreach mailboxes, grouped by domain.

## Who can use which mailbox

- **Gmail and Outlook accounts connected by signing in** are private to the person who connected them. Only they can send from them.
- **Mailboxes connected with a password, including outreach mailboxes Alva set up**, are shared. Anyone on the team can send from them and assign them to BDR agents.

Admins see every connection on the account and can reassign a mailbox to another team member. Other team members see only their own.

## What Alva reads

Once connected, Alva syncs your inbox and sent mail, matches each email to your contacts, companies and deals, and analyses it for sentiment, intent, buying signals, risks, open questions and commitments. Results appear on deal, contact and company pages. See [Understanding email analysis](/email/understanding-email-analysis).

Draft emails you're still writing are never imported.

## What Alva can send

With a connected account, you can:

- Send and reply from the **Inbox**, including AI-drafted replies
- Let [BDR agents](/bdr-agents/setting-up-your-first-agent) send outreach and follow-ups
- Attach your [email signature](/email/email-signatures) automatically

## Managing connections

From **Settings → Email Connections** you can:

- See when each account last synced, and click **Sync Now** to fetch new mail
- Click **Reconnect** when a sign-in has expired
- Disconnect an account — emails already synced stay in your CRM

Alva also syncs connected accounts automatically every 15 minutes.

<Warning>
  Email sync pauses when your credit balance reaches zero, and starts again automatically once you [add credits](/billing/buying-credits). The account stays connected in the meantime.
</Warning>

## Ask Alva

Alva can help with most mailbox housekeeping in chat:

- "Which mailboxes are connected?"
- "Change the sender name on sarah@getacme.com to Sarah Jones"
- "My emails aren't showing — can you sync my mailbox?"
- "Lower the daily limit on that mailbox to 5"

Alva can't connect a Gmail or Outlook account for you (you need to sign in yourself), disconnect a mailbox, or raise a daily limit. It will take you to **Settings → Email Connections** for those.

## Privacy

Email content is processed to produce the analysis, and the results are stored in your account. Your data is isolated to your account and isn't shared outside it. To stop Alva analysing mail from particular senders, add them to the **Email Ignore List** in **Settings → AI Settings**.
