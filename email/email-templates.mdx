Email templates are the building blocks of Template-mode outreach. They define what your [BDR agents](/bdr-agents/overview) send, and you can use them when writing emails yourself.

Go to **Templates** in the sidebar to manage your templates and groups.

## Template groups

Templates are organised into **groups** — think of each as a campaign or sequence. For example:

- "Cold Outreach — Healthcare"
- "Follow-Up Sequence"
- "Event Follow-Up"

To create a group, click **+** next to **Template Groups**, then give it a **Name**, an optional **Description** and a **Colour**. You assign groups to BDR agents, so keep each group focused on one purpose.

## Creating a template

Select a group and click **New**. Each template has:

- **Template Name** — for your reference, such as "Initial Outreach — CTO"
- **Group** — the group it belongs to, or **No group**
- **When to Use** — guidance that helps BDR agents pick the right template, such as "Use when you only have a generic address like info@"
- **Subject Line** and **Email Body**

Click **Create** (or **Save** when editing). The **Preview** tab shows the email with sample data filled in.

### Merge fields

Merge fields are replaced with real data when the email is sent. Click a field in the **Merge Fields** panel to insert it.

| Group | Fields |
|-------|--------|
| **Contact** | `{{contact.firstName}}`, `{{contact.lastName}}`, `{{contact.fullName}}`, `{{contact.jobTitle}}`, `{{contact.email}}` |
| **Company** | `{{company.name}}`, `{{company.industry}}`, `{{company.website}}` |
| **Sender** | `{{sender.name}}`, `{{sender.jobTitle}}`, `{{sender.email}}` |
| **Other** | `{{today}}` (e.g. 1 February 2026), `{{todayShort}}` (e.g. 01/02/26) |

**Custom fields.** If your account has custom fields on companies or contacts (set up in **Settings → Custom Fields**), they appear as **Company (custom)** and **Contact (custom)** groups, for example `{{company.custom.region}}`.

**Signal fields.** On accounts with [Signals](/discovery/signals), a **Signal** group adds `{{signal.hook}}` (what happened), `{{signal.date}}` (when it happened) and `{{signal.reference}}` (the organisation it was found through). They're filled from the company's latest signal in the last 45 days. Only use them in templates for agents working signal companies — anywhere else, the email is held back.

<Warning>
  If a merge field can't be filled — for example, the contact has no job title — the agent won't send the email.
</Warning>

### Spintax

Add variety by writing alternatives in curly brackets, separated by `|`:

```
{Hi|Hello|Hey} {{contact.firstName}},
```

Each email picks one option at random, so not every prospect gets identical wording.

## Spam score

Every template is scored from 0 to 100 for how likely it is to reach the inbox, each time you save it. Click **Spam Score** to check before saving.

| Score | Result |
|-------|--------|
| **85–100** | Saves — likely to reach the inbox |
| **70–84** | Saves, with warnings about spam risk |
| **Below 70** | **Won't save.** The findings show what to change, with a fix for each |

The template check can't see which mailbox will send it, so it's scored without a signature. When an agent sends, the email is scored again with the real signature, so that score can be lower. See [spam score on every email](/email/sending-health-and-limits#spam-score-on-every-email).

## Generating templates with AI

Click **AI Generate**, describe what the email should achieve, then choose a **Purpose** (such as Cold Outreach, Follow Up or Demo Request) and a **Tone**. Alva writes the template with suitable merge fields. If the first draft fails the spam check, Alva rewrites it before showing it to you.

## Variants

Variants are alternative versions of a template, each with a different approach, subject line and wording. They give BDR agents variety and let you see which angle works best.

1. Open a template
2. Click **Create Variants**
3. Choose how many you want (up to 20 at a time)
4. Review the new versions, each marked **Variant**, and delete any you don't like

Each variant is spam-checked like any other template.

## Sending a test

Click **Send Test**, choose the mailbox to send from and who to send it to. The test goes out as a real email so you can see it in your inbox.

## Using templates with BDR agents

When you [create a Template-mode agent](/bdr-agents/setting-up-your-first-agent), you assign one or more template groups to it. For each prospect, the agent picks the most suitable template from those groups (using your **When to Use** guidance), fills in the merge fields and sends it.

<Note>
  A Template-mode agent with no template groups can't send emails. Always assign at least one group.
</Note>

## Ask Alva

Alva can write, edit and organise templates in chat:

- "Write a cold outreach template for CTOs in healthcare"
- "Create three variants of my Initial Outreach template"
- "Move these templates into the Q4 Campaign group"
- "Send me a test of the follow-up template"

**Already have your own copy?** Paste the subject and body into the chat and ask Alva to save it as a template. It saves it word for word, without rewriting it. It still has to pass the spam check — if it doesn't, Alva shows you what to fix and offers to make the changes.
