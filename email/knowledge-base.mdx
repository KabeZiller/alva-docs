The Knowledge Base is your living sales playbook. It holds short, reusable pieces of sales knowledge — how to handle a common objection, a proof point that wins deals, what you will and won't do on price — so Alva and your [BDR agents](/bdr-agents/overview) give answers that match how your business actually sells.

Go to **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar.

## Where knowledge comes from

Alva builds the Knowledge Base for you from your real conversations:

- **Email threads** — when Alva [analyses your emails](/email/understanding-email-analysis), it looks for reusable patterns, such as a prospect's question and the answer that resolved it
- **Call transcripts** — recorded [calls](/calling/call-outcomes-and-insights) add up to two entries each, such as an objection and how it was handled
- **Your website** — when your [account is set up](/getting-started/account-setup), Alva adds company FAQs, use cases and pricing points from your site
- **Alva chat** — ask Alva to add something, such as "Add to the playbook: we never discount more than 15%"

<Note>
  **Product features belong in Products, not here.** Descriptions of what your product does go in [Products and competitors](/context/products-and-competitors). The Knowledge Base is for how you *sell* — and Alva keeps product brochure content out of it automatically.
</Note>

## Categories

| Category | What it covers |
|----------|----------------|
| **Objections** | Common concerns and how they were, or could be, handled |
| **Proof** | Proof points, results, case examples and return-on-investment figures |
| **Use cases** | Real-world ways customers use what you sell |
| **Pricing guardrail** | Price boundaries, discount norms and negotiation limits |
| **Competitor counter** | How to respond when a competitor comes up, and how you differ |
| **Process** | Next steps, procurement, onboarding — how buying from you works |
| **Company FAQ** | Short answers about your company that come up in conversation |
| **Other** | Reusable sales knowledge that doesn't fit elsewhere |

Use the category badges at the top of the list to filter, or the search box to find entries by meaning — "pricing objection" finds relevant entries even if they don't use those exact words.

## Entry statuses

Every entry has a status. Use the tabs to switch between them:

| Tab | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| **Active** | Trusted knowledge. **Only active entries are used by agents and drafted replies.** |
| **Provisional** | Newly found and not yet trusted. Waiting to be seen again or checked by a person. |
| **Disputed** | Clashes with an existing entry. Needs a human look. |
| **Superseded** | Retired. Kept for reference but no longer used. |

The page header shows how many entries are active against the limit — for example **112/150 active**.

## How entries become trusted

### Similar entries merge

When Alva finds something that matches an existing entry in the same category, it doesn't add a duplicate. It **reinforces** the existing entry: the evidence is added, and the count of times it's been seen goes up. If a new finding has the same title as an existing entry but says something different, the entry is marked **Disputed** so a person can decide which is right.

### Automatic activation

An entry is activated automatically when Alva can trust it, for example when:

- You've **pinned** it
- It comes from a trusted source — pricing and FAQs from your website, or something you asked Alva to add — and Alva is confident in it
- It's a pattern from a **won deal** that clearly worked
- Alva is highly confident in it **and** has seen it at least twice

Everything else waits as **Provisional** until it's seen again or you activate it.

### Trust signals on each entry

Each entry card shows:

- **Claim** — the short, usable line, with the detail underneath
- **Confidence** — how sure Alva is that this is a real insight
- **Corroboration** — how many times it's been seen (for example **×3 corroboration**)
- **Evidence** — click to see the conversations it came from
- **Resolution pattern** — for entries from email threads, the customer's question, your team's response and the outcome, with an effectiveness score where known
- When it was last reinforced, and who activated it

## Managing entries

On each entry you can:

- **Activate** — make a provisional or disputed entry active
- **Pin** — keep an entry active and protect it from being retired when the playbook is full
- **Edit** — change the title, claim or detail
- **Supersede** — retire an entry. Agents stop using it. There's no delete — superseding keeps the history

### The active limit

The playbook keeps up to about **150 active entries**, so agents work from a focused set rather than everything ever said. When the playbook is full and a new entry is activated, the least-corroborated unpinned entry is superseded to make room. Pin anything you always want kept.

## How your knowledge is used

When Alva or an agent needs an answer, it looks up the most relevant **active** entries:

- **BDR agents** use them when drafting replies to prospects
- **AI Draft** uses them when you reply to an email in the Inbox
- **Alva** uses them when you ask for help with an objection, competitor or pricing question, or ask her to write outreach

If nothing relevant is active, Alva says so and offers to add or activate an entry — she won't make one up.

## Ask Alva

Alva can browse and tidy the Knowledge Base for you:

- "How many Knowledge Base entries need review?"
- "Show me the provisional objection entries"
- "Activate all provisional pricing entries"
- "Supersede the competitor entries older than six months"
- "Pin the entry about our 30-day onboarding"
- "Add to the playbook: our typical payback period is under six months"

For changes to many entries at once, Alva first shows you which entries will change and whether any active entries would be retired to stay within the limit, then waits for your yes.

<Tip>
  Review the **Provisional** and **Disputed** tabs regularly, especially after closing deals. A few minutes activating good entries and superseding stale ones keeps your agents' answers sharp.
</Tip>
