---
title: "Email Signatures"
description: "Create signatures, choose which one each mailbox sends, and pick HTML or plain text."
---

Signatures are added to emails sent from a mailbox — by you, by Alva, or by a [BDR agent](/bdr-agents/overview). You can keep several signatures and choose which one each mailbox uses.

Signatures live in **Settings → Email Connections**, in the **Email Signatures** section.

## Creating a signature

Click **New Signature** and give it a **Signature Name**. Every signature has two versions, each on its own tab:

- **HTML** — the designed version, with formatting, links and images such as a logo
- **Plain text** — a text-only version, created from the HTML automatically. Edit it if you want different wording, or click **Regenerate from HTML** to start again
- **Preview** — shows both versions as they'll appear

## Merge fields

Insert these fields so one signature works on every mailbox:

| Field | What it inserts |
|-------|-----------------|
| `{{sender.name}}` | The mailbox's **Sender Name** |
| `{{sender.email}}` | The mailbox's email address |

Click the merge field buttons under the editor to insert them where your cursor is.

## The default signature

Click **Set Default** on a signature, or turn on **Default Signature** while editing it. The default is attached automatically to mailboxes you connect from then on, including new [outreach mailboxes](/email/outreach-mailboxes) Alva sets up for you. You can have one default at a time.

Mailboxes connected before you chose a default keep whatever they had. Assign a signature to them yourself, or ask Alva.

## Choosing a signature for each mailbox

Under **Connected Accounts**, each mailbox has a **Signature** row:

1. Pick the signature from the list, or **No signature**
2. Choose **HTML** or **Plain text** — which version this mailbox sends
3. Use the switch to turn signatures on or off for the mailbox. Turn it off if you'd rather your email app adds its own signature

<Tip>
  **Use plain text for cold outreach mailboxes.** A logo and several links on a short first email look like bulk mail to spam filters, especially on a new domain. Keep the plain-text version to your name, title, company and at most one link. HTML is fine for your personal mailbox and for replies once a conversation is under way. Outreach mailboxes Alva sets up use plain text by default.
</Tip>

Signatures count towards the [spam score](/email/sending-health-and-limits#spam-score-on-every-email) of every agent email. If an agent's emails score badly because of the signature, switching that mailbox to plain text usually fixes it.

## Testing a signature

Send yourself a real email to check how a signature looks in your inbox:

- While editing a signature, click **Send Test**, choose the mailbox to send from, the recipient, and the **Signature version** (HTML or plain text)
- On a mailbox under **Connected Accounts**, click **Test** to send its current signature

## Ask Alva to design one

Alva can design a signature for you, using your company name, logo and website:

- "Create an email signature for our outreach mailboxes"
- "Make the plain-text version shorter — just name, title and website"
- "Put the new signature on all three getacme.com mailboxes, plain text"
- "Which mailboxes don't have a signature?"

Alva describes the layout and checks with you before saving. Before assigning a signature, it lists each mailbox and what it has now, and asks which ones should change.
