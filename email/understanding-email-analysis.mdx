When you [connect your email](/email/connecting-an-email-account), Alva reads each business conversation automatically, links it to the right contact, company and deal, and pulls out what matters for your pipeline. This page explains what Alva looks for and where the results appear.

## Which emails are analysed

Alva focuses on business conversations, not everything in your inbox:

- **Emails from people already in your CRM** are always analysed.
- **Replies to your outreach** are always analysed, even a one-line "thanks, not now" from a different address at the same company.
- **Everything else** gets a quick relevance check first. Newsletters, marketing emails, automated notifications, password resets and vendors pitching *to you* are skipped. Genuine business enquiries, meeting requests, proposals and partner emails go through to full analysis.

If someone at a company already in your CRM emails you for the first time, Alva can add them as a new contact at that company. Alva never creates new companies from your inbox.

### How emails are matched

Alva links each email to your records by email address — first an exact match on a contact or company address, then by the sender's company domain. So a reply from `jane@acme.com` is linked to Acme even if Jane isn't a contact yet. Free webmail addresses such as Gmail or Outlook aren't matched by domain, because many unrelated people share them.

### Ignoring senders

To stop Alva analysing mail from particular people — personal contacts, a newsletter that slips through, or an automated sender — add their address to the **Email Ignore List** in **Settings → AI Settings**. Emails from those addresses are still synced, but no analysis, knowledge extraction or CRM updates happen.

The Email Ignore List doesn't affect outreach. To stop agents emailing someone, use the [Email Block List](/email/email-block-list).

## What Alva looks for

Alva reads emails you receive and emails you send differently, because the same words mean different things depending on who wrote them.

### Emails from prospects

**Sentiment** — positive, neutral, negative or mixed. Tracked over time, so you can see whether a deal's tone is improving.

**Intent** — what the sender is trying to do:

| Intent | Example |
|--------|---------|
| **Inquiry** | Asking for information |
| **Objection** | Pushing back or raising a concern |
| **Negotiation** | Discussing terms, price or scope |
| **Meeting request** | Asking to meet or call |
| **Follow-up** | Chasing or continuing an earlier conversation |
| **Update** | Sharing news or a change in status |
| **Referral** | Pointing you to someone else — "you should speak to our Head of IT" |
| **Auto-reply** | Out-of-office messages and automatic acknowledgements |
| **Other** | Anything else |

**Urgency** — low, medium or high, with the reason.

**Buying signals** — signs the prospect is moving towards a purchase, tagged as budget, authority, timeline, need, decision process or champion.

**Risk signals** — signs the deal could be in trouble, such as a competitor mention, budget concern, timeline delay, change of stakeholder, objection or the prospect going quiet.

**Questions** — every question or concern that needs an answer from you.

**Commitments** — things the prospect has promised to do, and when.

Alva also picks up:

- **Referrals** — the name, email and role of the person you've been pointed to
- **Out-of-office replies** — including the return date, so follow-ups can wait
- **Do-not-contact requests** — "remove me", "unsubscribe", "please don't email us again". The person is blocked from future outreach automatically

### Emails from your team

For emails you send, Alva checks how well you're moving the deal forward:

- **Intent** — response, proposal, follow-up, meeting request, update, introduction or other
- **Questions addressed** — which of the prospect's open questions this email answers, fully or partly
- **Your commitments** — what you've promised to do, and by when
- **Risks handled** — which open risks the email deals with
- **New questions** — what you've asked the prospect

When you answer a prospect's question, Alva marks it as addressed, so only genuinely unanswered questions stay open.

### Methodology evidence

If your deal uses a [sales methodology](/crm/sales-methodologies) (MEDDPICC, BANT or SPICED), Alva also looks for evidence for each field — for example, a prospect describing their pain point is MEDDPICC "Identified Pain" evidence. Suggestions appear on the deal for you to review and apply with one click.

## Where results appear

### On deal pages

The **Email Intelligence** panel on each deal summarises everything from the deal's emails:

- **Open Questions to Address**
- **Buying Signals**
- **Active Risks**
- **Pending Commitments** — yours and the prospect's, with overdue ones flagged
- **Methodology Updates Available**

### In the Inbox

Go to **Inbox** in the sidebar to see your conversations, grouped by thread. Open an email and click **AI Analysis** to see what Alva found. Emails Alva judged not relevant are hidden by default — you can choose to show them.

### On contact and company pages

Contact and company pages list every email involving that person or company, with links to the related deal.

## Emails are read in order

Alva processes emails in each thread in the order they were sent, so it always knows what was said earlier. That's how it can tell when a later email has answered an earlier question.

## How it feeds into the rest of Alva

- **Deal health** — positive sentiment, active engagement and answered questions raise a deal's [health score](/crm/managing-deals); risks, negative sentiment and long silences lower it.
- **Knowledge Base** — useful patterns, such as how your team handled an objection, are added to your [Knowledge Base](/email/knowledge-base).
- **BDR agents** — replies to outreach are classified so agents can [hand over genuine interest](/bdr-agents/managing-replies), wait out an out-of-office, or stop when someone asks to be removed.

## Ask Alva

Alva can use the analysis in chat:

- "What's the latest with Acme? Summarise the email thread"
- "Which questions from Globex haven't we answered?"
- "Are there any risks on the Initech deal?"
- "Draft a reply to Jane's last email"

When you ask for a draft reply, Alva uses the whole thread, the deal and company details, and your [Knowledge Base](/email/knowledge-base), and saves the draft in the thread ready for you to check and send. You can also click **AI Draft** when replying in the Inbox.
