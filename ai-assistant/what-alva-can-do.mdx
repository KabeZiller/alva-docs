Alva can do almost everything the app can do. Behind the scenes she works with a team of specialists — one for your CRM records, one for Discovery, one for BDR agents, one for email setup, one for billing and so on — and hands each request to the right one. You just ask in plain English, in the [chat panel](/ai-assistant/talking-to-alva) or [by email](/ai-assistant/alva-by-email).

## How Alva keeps you in control

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="She asks before anything important" icon="hand">
    Anything that **spends money**, **contacts people outside your company** or **deletes something** waits for your yes. Alva tells you what she's about to do first.
  </Card>
  <Card title="She follows your permissions" icon="shield-check">
    Alva acts as you. If your role can't change a setting in the app, Alva can't change it for you either.
  </Card>
  <Card title="She drafts, you send" icon="pen">
    Alva writes emails and replies as drafts. She sends only test emails you've approved — your BDR agents do the sending once you've switched them on.
  </Card>
  <Card title="She sends you to the right page" icon="signs-post">
    When something has to happen in the browser, Alva explains why and takes you there.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

| What you can ask | Example |

| **Contracts and recurring revenue** | "Record a £20k expansion on the Acme contract" |
| **Activities** | "Log a call with Sarah — discussed the implementation timeline" |
| **Tags and custom fields** | "Tag these five companies as 'Event leads'" |

| **Search everything** | "Search everything for 'pricing proposal'" |

Alva cancels a contract only after you confirm. To import a spreadsheet of companies, attach the CSV: Alva tells you what's in it and imports it after your yes. For large files she sends you to [Importing data](/crm/importing-data).

## Discovery and prospecting

| What you can ask | Example |
|------------------|---------|
| **Company searches** | "Find marketing agencies in Manchester" |
| **Funded and high-growth companies** | "Find Series B fintechs in London" |
| **People searches** | "Find heads of operations at UK logistics firms" |
| **Run and tune Search Agents** | "Pause the healthcare search", "Raise the match threshold to 70" |
| **Review results** | "Import the ten best matches", "Discard everything below 40" |
| **Enrichment** | "Enrich Acme and look for their heads of marketing" |
| **Signals** (Pro, closed beta) | "Tell me when a London fintech raises a Series A" |
| **Pulse** (Pro) | "What's in today's Pulse?", "Track this competitor's website" |

Alva picks the right kind of [Search Agent](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile) for what you describe — an area-based company search, a [funded & high-growth](/discovery/company-intelligence) search or a [people search](/discovery/people-search) — and asks a quick question when it could be more than one. She checks first that a search makes sense and tells you roughly how many results to expect.

Alva never switches on [Tier 2 contact search](/discovery/enrichment-profiles#tier-2-contact-search) or mobile-number reveal unless you ask for it.

## BDR agents

Alva can build, preview, start, tune and pause [BDR agents](/bdr-agents/overview) in both **Template** and **Personalised** mode.

| What you can ask | Example |
|------------------|---------|
| **Create an agent** | "Set up a Personalised agent for my healthcare search" |
| **Build the playbook** | "Add an offer about our compliance tooling and keep the tone warm" |
| **Preview the emails** | "Show me what it would send to three companies" |
| **Start and pause** | "Start the Healthcare Prospector", "Pause it" |
| **Find out why it isn't sending** | "Why hasn't the healthcare agent sent anything today?" |
| **See what it's doing** | "What has the agent done this week, and why?" |

If you just say "set up a BDR agent", Alva asks whether you want **Template** or **Personalised** first. Every new agent starts paused, and starting it is a separate step.

<Note>
  **Alva is the recommended way to set up a [Personalised](/bdr-agents/personalised-outreach) agent.** She builds the playbook with you, previews real emails without sending anything, and only lets the agent go live once its checklist passes. On new accounts, starting an agent needs Pro — building, editing and previewing stay open.
</Note>

## Calling

Alva can search for and set up [phone numbers](/calling/phone-numbers) (admins, after you confirm), manage call forwarding and call scripts, build [calling sessions](/calling/calling-sessions) (Pro) from your BDR agents, the Lead Sheet, tags or named contacts, and answer questions about the Call Log — "what did Sarah say on yesterday's call?". She never places calls herself.

## Advertising and landing pages

Alva can plan and draft [LinkedIn Lead Gen campaigns](/advertising/creating-a-campaign), make and edit [ad images](/advertising/ad-images), build and publish [landing pages](/advertising/landing-pages), and report on [leads and results](/advertising/leads-and-results). Say "I want to advertise" and she proposes one plan based on your profile, products and website. She drafts the campaign, images and landing page only after your yes. Connecting your LinkedIn ad account happens in the browser — Alva sends you to [Connecting LinkedIn](/advertising/connecting-linkedin).

## Your business context

## Agenda and weekly report

Alva keeps an agenda of her own: reminders you give her, follow-ups on things you promised, and reviews of Search Agents and BDR agents she's helped set running. Where it's switched on, she also sends a [weekly business development report](/ai-assistant/agenda-and-weekly-report) with numbered recommendations you can approve by replying "yes to #2".

## Getting around

Alva can take you to any page in the app — "take me to the Lead Sheet", "open Acme Corp", "go to billing". She also knows what each page does, so she can tell you where to find something.

## What Alva sends you to a page for

A few things have to happen in your browser. Alva explains why and takes you to the right page (or, by email, sends you a one-time link):

- **Connecting accounts** — Gmail or Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or Outlook, and your LinkedIn ad account
- **Entering a card** — on the secure card page
- **Uploading files** — such as verification documents for phone numbers, or company images
- **Creating an API key** — so you can copy it once in **Settings → Developer**
- **Deleting a BDR agent** — Alva offers to pause it instead

## Background work Alva does for you

- **Deal health scoring** — every deal gets a health score that stays current on deal cards and pages.
- **Risk alerts** — when a deal goes quiet or its health drops, you get a notification.
- **Email analysis** — Alva reads your synced emails for sentiment, buying signals and evidence for your deals. See [Understanding email analysis](/email/understanding-email-analysis).

You choose which of these run in [AI settings](/ai-assistant/ai-settings).

<Tip>
  If you ask for something Alva can't do yet, she records it so the product team can see what people need — and points you to where you can do it today.
</Tip>
