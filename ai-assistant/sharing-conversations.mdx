---
title: "Sharing conversations"
description: "Share an Alva conversation with chosen colleagues or your whole team, so they can read it and join in."
---

Every conversation with Alva is private to you until you share it. Sharing lets colleagues read the whole conversation and carry it on with Alva — useful for handing over a deal analysis, working through a campaign plan together, or showing your manager how you set up a BDR agent.

## How to share a conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Open the Alva panel on the conversation you want to share. Use **Chat history** to find an older one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Share">
    Click the **Share** button in the panel header. It appears once the conversation has started, and only for the person who started it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can see it">
    Pick one of the three options below. For **Specific people**, tick the team members you want to include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The people you've shared with are notified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sharing options

| Option | Who can see and take part |
|--------|---------------------------|
| **Private** | Only you. Every conversation starts here |
| **Specific people** | You and the team members you choose |
| **Everyone in team** | Everyone on your account |

You can change the option at any time — open **Share** again and pick another. Choosing **Private** stops sharing.

## What the people you share with can do

People you've shared a conversation with find it under **Shared with me** in their **Chat history**. They can read everything in it — your messages, Alva's replies and any tables, reports or documents it produced — and send messages to Alva in the same conversation. When they do, Alva acts with *their* permissions, not yours.

Only the person who started a conversation can change who it's shared with.

## Email threads with Alva

When you or a colleague [email Alva](/ai-assistant/alva-by-email), the email thread is saved as a conversation too. It appears in the sender's **Chat history** with everything Alva did, and can be shared in the same way.

## Privacy

Shared conversations never leave your account. Only signed-in members of your team can open them, and only if the conversation has been shared with them.
