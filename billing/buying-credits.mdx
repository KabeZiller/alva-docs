---
title: "Buying credits"
description: "Top up your balance by hand, or let auto top-up keep it topped up within a monthly limit you set."
---

There are two ways to add credit: buy it when you need it, or switch on **auto top-up** so your balance never runs dry. Credit you buy **never expires**.

## Buying credit now

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing">
    Go to **Settings → Billing & Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Purchase Credits">
    Click **Purchase Credits** on the **Credit Balance** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an amount">
    Pick a preset or use the slider. The cost in your billing currency is shown before you pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Complete payment on the secure card page. The credit is added to your balance straight away, and your card is saved so auto top-up can use it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Auto top-up

Auto top-up buys credit for you whenever your balance runs low, so Search Agents, BDR agents and Alva keep working. You set three things on the **Auto Top-Up** card at **Settings → Billing & Subscription**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Top up when balance falls below** | The balance that triggers a top-up |
| **Top-up amount** | How much is charged each time |
| **Maximum monthly auto top-up spend** | The most auto top-up can charge in a calendar month. Once it's reached, auto top-up stops until next month |

The card sums it up in one sentence — when your balance drops below one amount, a set amount is charged to your card, up to your monthly limit — and shows what auto top-up has spent so far this month. The monthly limit must be at least as much as one top-up.

### Switching it on

Auto top-up needs a saved card.

- **No card saved yet:** choose your amounts, then click **Save card and switch on**. You save your card on the secure card page, and auto top-up is switched on with the amounts you chose. Your card is used only for these top-ups, never for more than your monthly limit. Only admins can do this.
- **Card already saved:** use the switch at the top of the card, adjust the amounts and click **Save Settings**.

If your balance is already below the threshold when you save, the first top-up is charged straight away. If this month's limit has been used, the card says so; top-ups resume next month, or as soon as you raise the limit and save.

<Tip>
  Auto top-up is the simplest way to run Alva on Pay as you go: spend follows your activity, and the monthly limit keeps it predictable.
</Tip>

## Buying credit through Alva

Ask Alva — "top up our credit" or "raise our auto top-up limit" — and she:

- tells you what's available and what it costs, and buys it after your yes. With a saved card, it's charged straight away; without one, she sends you to the secure card page.
- changes your auto top-up amounts after your yes. If there's no saved card, she explains how to add one.
- by email, sends admins a one-time billing link that opens the card and auto top-up setup. Billing links expire after an hour. See [Alva by Email](/ai-assistant/alva-by-email#action-links).

## Checking your balance

Your balance, in your billing currency, is always on the **Credit Balance** card at **Settings → Billing & Subscription**. The card shows:

- what's **available** — or, on a new account, how much **starter credit** is left
- on Pro, your monthly credit and when it renews
- any credit you've **purchased** on top

For a breakdown of where credit goes, see [Understanding credits](/billing/understanding-credits#seeing-where-credit-goes).

<Note>
  If you're buying credit every month and your spending is steady, Pro may work out cheaper. Ask Alva "would Pro save us money?" — see [Plans and pricing](/billing/plans-and-pricing#is-pro-worth-it-for-you).
</Note>
