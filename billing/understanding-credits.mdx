You pay for what Alva does. Every piece of AI work — a reply in chat, an enriched company, an email a BDR agent writes — uses a little credit from your account's balance. Spend rises when your campaigns are busy and falls when things are quiet, and you decide the ceiling.

## Your balance is shown in money

Your credit balance is shown in your billing currency — pounds, dollars or euros — not in points or units. You'll find it on the **Credit Balance** card at **Settings → Billing & Subscription**.

## How new accounts start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Starter credit">
    Every new account starts on **Pay as you go** with a starter credit, so you can try Alva properly without a card. There's no time limit — use it at your own pace. The Credit Balance card shows how much starter credit is left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A saved card with auto top-up">
    When you're ready, save a card and switch on [auto top-up](/billing/buying-credits#auto-top-up). Your balance is topped up automatically when it runs low, never beyond the monthly limit you set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pro, when it pays">
    If you use Alva heavily, [Pro](/billing/plans-and-pricing) gives you more work for your money and opens extra features. Alva tells you when your own spending says Pro would save you money.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When credit runs out

If your balance runs out, Alva's work pauses cleanly — nothing fails halfway through or is lost. Search Agents, BDR agents and Alva's own scheduled work wait until there's credit again, then carry on.

Admins get **one** email about it. If there's no card on the account, the email has an **Add a card** link that sets up your card, monthly limit and auto top-up in one step. The dashboard shows the same message with an **Add a card** button.

## What uses credit

| Area | What uses credit |
|------|------------------|
| **Alva** | Chat conversations, emails to Alva, and her scheduled agenda work |
| **Discovery** | Each company a Search Agent finds |
| **Enrichment** | Finding contacts and email addresses for a company, cleaning up the data and scoring how well it matches your search |
| **People searches and contact details** | Each verified email a people search reveals, and any mobile numbers or contact details you reveal |
| **BDR agents** | The AI decisions an agent makes about each company, the emails it writes and sends, and the safety check on each email before it goes |
| **Email** | Email analysis, AI reply drafts, spam scoring and inbox placement tests |
| **Signals and Pulse** | Each search, preview and run |
| **Calling** | Call minutes, recordings and transcription, plus the AI analysis after each call |
| **Advertising** | Each ad image Alva makes or edits |
| **Background AI** | Deal health scoring, risk alerts and the other features in [AI settings](/ai-assistant/ai-settings) |

Alva asks before anything that costs a noticeable amount, such as revealing contact details or running a placement test, and tells you what it will cost.

<Note>
  Recurring charges for things like phone numbers and outreach mailboxes are billed separately as [add-ons](/billing/add-ons), not taken from your credit.
</Note>

## What's included on every plan, and what needs Pro

Everyday enrichment — finding contacts and emails for the companies you import, and scoring them against your search — runs on every plan, paid in credit like everything else.

These features need **Pro**:

- [Tier 2 contact search](/discovery/enrichment-profiles#tier-2-contact-search), and revealing contact emails and mobile numbers
- [Pulse](/discovery/pulse)
- [Signals](/discovery/signals) (closed beta)
- [Calling sessions](/calling/calling-sessions)
- Starting a BDR agent, on new accounts. Building, editing and previewing agents are open to everyone

## Keeping spending predictable

- **Feature budgets** — set a monthly limit for Search Agents, BDR agents and Pulse in [AI settings](/ai-assistant/ai-settings#feature-budgets). Each pauses when it reaches its limit.
- **BDR agent budgets** — each agent has its own monthly budget. See [Configuring budgets and hours](/bdr-agents/configuring-budgets-and-hours).
- **Auto top-up limit** — auto top-up never charges more than your monthly limit.
- **Alva's agenda caps** — daily and per-run caps on Alva's scheduled work, at **Settings → Alva Agenda**.

## Seeing where credit goes

- **Settings → Billing & Subscription** shows your balance and a **Usage – Last 30 Days** chart by feature, including the average cost of each company discovered and enriched.
- **Settings → Usage** lists every request with the feature, a description and its cost.

You can also ask Alva — "what have we spent this month?" or "what's using the most credit?".

<Tip>
  If credit is going faster than you expected, check the usage chart. Search Agents and BDR agents are usually the biggest users. Lower a feature budget or pause a search while you decide.
</Tip>
