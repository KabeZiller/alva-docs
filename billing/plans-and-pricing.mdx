Alva has two plans for new customers. **Pay as you go** has no monthly fee: you pay only for the credit you use. **Pro** has a monthly fee that comes back to you as credit each month, at a better usage rate, and opens extra features.

## The two plans

| | **Pay as you go** | **Pro** |
|--|-------------------|---------|
| **Monthly fee** | None | Yes — returned to you as credit every month |
| **Usage rate** | Standard | Better: the same work costs less credit |
| **How you pay for usage** | Starter credit, then a saved card with [auto top-up](/billing/buying-credits#auto-top-up) | Your monthly credit, then top-ups or auto top-up if you need more |
| **Pro features** | — | Included |
| **Best for** | Getting started, and lighter or seasonal use | Teams using Alva heavily every month |

Every new account starts on **Pay as you go** with a [starter credit](/billing/understanding-credits#how-new-accounts-start).

### What Pro unlocks

- [Tier 2 contact search](/discovery/enrichment-profiles#tier-2-contact-search), and revealing contact emails and mobile numbers
- [Pulse](/discovery/pulse)
- [Signals](/discovery/signals) (closed beta)
- [Calling sessions](/calling/calling-sessions)
- Starting BDR agents, on new accounts

Everything else — Search Agents, everyday enrichment, the CRM, building, editing and previewing BDR agents, advertising and landing pages — works on both plans.

<Note>
  On new accounts, a BDR agent needs Pro to **start sending**. You can build it, write its playbook and preview its emails on Pay as you go; the BDR Agents pages show **Upgrade to Pro to start** where you'd normally start the agent.
</Note>

## Is Pro worth it for you?

Pro pays for itself once your monthly spend is high enough. Ask Alva "would Pro save us money?" and she compares your own spending over the last 30 days with what the same work would cost on Pro. She recommends Pro only when your figures say it would save you money, and tells you the saving and the monthly spend at which Pro breaks even. Otherwise she'll tell you Pay as you go is the right fit.

Alva doesn't make this recommendation while you're still on your starter credit or in your first two weeks, because there isn't enough usage to go on yet.

## Changing your plan

### Moving to Pro

Go to **Settings → Billing & Subscription** and click **Upgrade Plan** on the **Current Plan** card, then **Select Plan** on Pro. You complete payment on the secure card page and Pro starts straight away.

You can also ask Alva — "move us to Pro". She confirms the price first and then sends you to checkout, or by email sends you a one-time billing link.

### Switching between paid plans

On a paid plan, the Current Plan card shows **Change Plan**. A switch takes effect immediately: you're charged the pro-rated difference straight away and your monthly credit is reset for the new plan.

### Leaving a paid plan

Cancel through **Manage Billing**. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period — the card shows **Cancelling** and the date it ends — and the account then moves to Pay as you go. You can reactivate from the billing portal before then.

<Warning>
  Monthly plan credit doesn't roll over from one month to the next. Credit you've bought separately never expires and stays on your account if you change plan.
</Warning>

## Manage Billing

On a paid plan, click **Manage Billing** on the Current Plan card to open the billing portal, where you can:

- update your card
- view and download invoices
- cancel or reactivate your subscription

Admins can also ask Alva for a one-time link to the billing portal by email.

## Billing currency

Alva bills in **pounds (GBP)**, **US dollars (USD)** or **euros (EUR)**. Your currency is set when you sign up and is used for prices, credit and invoices throughout the app. You can't change currency while you're on a paid plan.

All prices exclude applicable tax, such as VAT, which is calculated at checkout from your billing address.
