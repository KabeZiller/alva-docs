title: "Working With Your Results"
description: "Review the companies your Search Agents find, import the good ones, and discard the rest."
---

Once a Search Agent is active, the companies it finds start arriving in your **Discovery Pipeline**. This page explains how to review them, decide which to keep, and move them into your CRM.

## Where results appear

| Page | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Discovery → Companies** (the Discovery Pipeline) | Every company your searches have found. New ones wait at the **Found** stage for review; imported ones move through your pipeline stages |
| **Discovery → Contacts** | People captured by your searches, such as those found by [People Search](/discovery/people-search) and [Signals](/discovery/signals). See [Discovery Contacts](/discovery/discovery-contacts) |
| **Outreach → Lead Sheet** | Leads that need a human — replies, drafts, escalations, form fills. See [Lead Sheet](/discovery/lead-sheet) |

## Reviewing companies at the Found stage

Go to **Discovery → Companies** and choose a tab — **Customers**, **Vendors**, **Partners**, or **Suppliers** — then click the **Found** card. Found companies are the ones waiting for your decision: they're not in your CRM yet.

Each company shows:

- **Company** — name, location, and website
- **Match** — its match score as a percentage
- **Contacts** — how many people were captured with it
- **Search Agent** — which search found it
- **Discovered** — when it was found

Found companies are sorted by **Best Match** first. You can also sort by **Name** or **Date Added**, and use the Search Agent filter to see one search at a time.

### Import or discard one at a time

Open the menu on any row and choose:

- **Import to CRM** — the company becomes a full CRM record at the first stage of its pipeline (for example **Target** for customers), and [enrichment](/discovery/enrichment) starts.
- **Discard** — the company is removed from your review list.
- **View details** — see the company and why it was scored as it was.

You can also tick several companies and click **Import Selected**.

### Bulk actions by score

The **Bulk Actions** card lets you clear the Found stage in two clicks. Set the **Confidence Threshold** slider, then:

- **Import N Above X%** — imports every Found company scoring at or above the threshold
- **Discard N Below X%** — discards every Found company scoring below it

The buttons show how many companies each would affect before you click. If you've filtered to one Search Agent, they only act on that search's companies.

### Review Mode

For a closer look, filter the Found stage to one Search Agent and click **Review Mode**. It shows one company at a time beside your Search Agent's description, so you can:

- See the company's details and the reasoning behind its score
- Edit the description to improve future matches
- Change the match threshold without leaving the page
- Move quickly with keyboard shortcuts: **J** next, **K** previous, **I** import, **D** discard, **Esc** to exit

Area-based Search Agents pause themselves after their first 50 companies and invite you into Review Mode. See [Paused for review](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile#paused-for-review).

## Match scores

Alva scores each company 0–100 against your Search Agent's description and groups it using the agent's **match threshold**:

| Result | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Match** | At or above your threshold — a good fit |
| **Worth a look** | Within about 20 points below your threshold — a partial fit worth checking |
| **Not a match** | Well below your threshold — probably not what you're after |

Raise the match threshold on the Search Agent to be stricter, or lower it to see more companies. A higher threshold means fewer but more relevant matches.

<Note>
  For People Search and Funded & high-growth searches, the score only sets priority — no company is held back because of it. On every stage of the Discovery Pipeline, companies open sorted by match score, highest first, with unscored companies at the end.
</Note>

## Auto-import

On an area-based Search Agent, you can turn on **auto-import**: companies at or above the match threshold go straight into your CRM at the Target stage, and those below it are discarded. Useful when you trust the description and want to move fast. See [Auto-import](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile#auto-import).

People Search and Funded & high-growth searches import every company they find automatically, so their companies skip the Found stage.

## Duplicate detection

Alva matches companies on their **domain** when importing. If a company already exists in your CRM, the search result is linked to the existing record instead of creating a second one. You can run several Search Agents and import from all of them without creating duplicates.

## After import

Imported companies move through your pipeline on the Discovery Pipeline — see [Discovery Companies](/discovery/discovery-companies). Once [enrichment](/discovery/enrichment) has finished, a [BDR Agent](/bdr-agents/overview) linked to the Search Agent can start outreach.

Companies with no website show a **No website** badge instead of an enrichment status. There's no domain to search for more contacts, but they can still be worked by a BDR Agent using the contact you already have.

## Costs

Each company a Search Agent finds uses credits, whether you import or discard it — the cost covers the search and the matching. See **Settings → Usage** for a breakdown.

<Tip>
  Start with a small geography and a well-written description. Review the first batch, refine the description or categories if needed, then expand to more locations.
</Tip>

You can also ask Alva: *"Import every company above 75% from my healthcare search."* Alva tells you how many companies that would import and waits for your yes before doing it.
