The **Discovery Pipeline** shows every company your Search Agents have found, from the moment it's found to the point it becomes a lead. Use it to review new companies, move them through your pipeline, and see how outreach is going.

## Opening the pipeline

Go to **Discovery → Companies** in the sidebar.

## Relationship tabs

Tabs across the top split the pipeline by relationship — **Customers**, **Vendors**, **Partners**, and **Suppliers** — each with its own count. The relationship comes from the Search Agent's [profile type](/discovery/profile-types-explained). Competitors found by a Competitor Profile go to **Context → Competitors** instead.

## Stage cards

Under the tabs, a row of cards shows how many companies are at each stage. Click a card to see that stage.

| Tab | Stages |
|-----|--------|
| Customers | Found → Target → Prospect → Lead |
| Vendors | Found → Target → Evaluating → Negotiating |
| Partners | Found → Target → Engaging → Negotiating |
| Suppliers | Found → Target → Evaluating → Negotiating |

- **Found** — companies waiting for your review. They're not in your CRM yet.
- **Target** — imported into your CRM, no outreach yet.
- **Prospect** (or **Evaluating** / **Engaging**) — outreach has started.
- **Lead** (or **Negotiating**) — the company is ready for active sales engagement and also appears on the [Lead Sheet](/discovery/lead-sheet).

## The Found stage

The Found stage is where you decide what to keep. You can **Import** or **Discard** companies one by one, in bulk by score, or in **Review Mode**. Import is only available here — once a company is imported it moves on to the next stage. See [Working with your results](/discovery/working-with-your-results) for the full review workflow.

## Imported stages

On every other stage you'll see the companies already in your CRM:

- **Company** — name and website
- **Match** — match score, where the search scored it
- **Contacts** — how many contacts the company has
- **Emails** — how many outreach emails have been sent (from Prospect onwards)
- **Enrichment** — its [enrichment status](/discovery/enrichment#enrichment-status), including **No website** for companies without one
- **Stage** and when it was **Discovered**

Companies open sorted by **match score**, highest first, with unscored companies at the end. You can also sort by name, date added, or stage.

### Filtering and searching

- Use the **Search Agent** filter to see companies from one search.
- Use the search box to find companies by name, domain, or industry.

### Bulk actions

At the **Target** and **Prospect** stages, tick companies to:

- **Add to Enrichment Queue** — enrich (or re-enrich) the selected companies
- **Add Tags** — apply [tags](/crm/tags) to all of them at once
- **Delete Selected** — remove them from your CRM and mark the search results as discarded

## Moving companies through stages

Use **Update Stage** in a row's menu, or open the company and use its lifecycle stage selector. When a prospect replies to a BDR Agent's outreach, the company moves to **Lead** automatically (**Negotiating** for vendors, partners, and suppliers).

## A company's contacts

Open a company to see **every contact** it has. If the company came from a [People Search](/discovery/people-search), the person that search found is listed first, followed by the contacts enrichment found afterwards, in order of seniority.

## Relationship to BDR Agents

A [BDR Agent](/bdr-agents/overview) linked to a Search Agent works through that search's imported companies once they've been [enriched](/discovery/enrichment), best match first. You can see which companies it has contacted from the **Emails** count, and companies that need a human appear on the [Lead Sheet](/discovery/lead-sheet).
