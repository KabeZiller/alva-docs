---
title: "Enrichment Profiles"
description: "Reusable contact targeting for companies you import yourself — plus deeper Tier 2 sourcing on the Pro plan."
---

When a company comes from a Search Agent, [enrichment](/discovery/enrichment) uses that agent's settings to decide **who** to find. But when you bring companies in yourself — a CSV upload, a single company added by hand — there's no Search Agent behind them to say who matters.

An **Enrichment Profile** fills that gap: a named, reusable set of contact-targeting settings you can link to any company so enrichment knows who to look for.

## When to use one

- You've **imported companies from a spreadsheet** and want them enriched with the right contacts.
- You **added a company by hand** and want Alva to find its decision-makers.
- You want to apply the **same targeting repeatedly** across different batches without retyping it.

<Note>
  Companies found by a Search Agent already carry that agent's targeting, so they don't need an Enrichment Profile.
</Note>

## Creating an Enrichment Profile

Go to **Discovery → Search Agents** and click **Add Enrichment Profile**. Give it a clear name (e.g. "UK Fintech Decision-Makers") and set your targeting in the **People** section:

| Setting | What it controls |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Enable Enrichment** | Whether contacts are found for linked companies |
| **Primary Job Functions** | The roles to prioritise (e.g. Head of Marketing, VP Sales) |
| **Secondary Job Functions** | Fallback roles if the primary ones aren't found |
| **Economic Buyer** | The ideal decision-maker title (e.g. CEO, CFO, Managing Director) — these contacts are flagged and prioritised |
| **Tier 2 Contact Search** | Optional deeper sourcing on the Pro plan — see [below](#tier-2-contact-search) |

An Enrichment Profile holds targeting only — it never searches for companies. It appears in your Search Agents list with an **Enrichment** badge, so it's easy to find and edit.

## Linking a profile to a company

Open the company's page. Companies you added yourself show an enrichment profile selector beside the enrichment badge:

1. Choose the Enrichment Profile from the list (or **Create an enrichment profile** if you don't have one yet).
2. Click **Run Enrichment** (or **Retry Enrichment** if it has run before).

The company then goes through the standard [enrichment process](/discovery/enrichment) — finding contacts, cleaning the data, ranking by seniority, and flagging your economic buyer.

You can also ask Alva: *"Link my UK Fintech Decision-Makers profile to Acme Ltd and enrich it."*

## Tier 2 Contact Search

Base enrichment finds the contacts and emails associated with a company's website, and runs on every plan. **Tier 2 Contact Search** is an optional, deeper layer that finds **more** contacts and reveals **more verified emails** — and, if you choose, **mobile numbers** and direct dials.

<Warning>
  Tier 2 adds a **per-contact cost** on top of base enrichment, and mobile numbers cost more than emails. It's **off by default** and only runs when you switch it on. Base enrichment alone is enough for most outreach.
</Warning>

When you switch Tier 2 on, you choose:

- **Reveal email addresses** — find more verified emails
- **Reveal mobile numbers** — add direct mobile numbers
- **Only for companies matching at least** a match score — so you only pay for deeper sourcing on your best fits. Leave it blank to run Tier 2 for every company.

<Note>
  While a minimum match score is set, companies **without** a score are skipped too. If you later **lower** the minimum on a Search Agent, the **People** card offers **Run people search again**, which runs Tier 2 for already-imported companies that now qualify. It doesn't repeat the base email search, and new emails and mobiles are charged as usual.
</Note>

<Note>
  Tier 2 Contact Search needs the **Pro plan**. On other plans the Tier 2 option is locked with the message "Base enrichment still runs" — every other People setting works as normal.
</Note>

## Managing with Alva

You can create and apply Enrichment Profiles by asking [Alva](/ai-assistant/talking-to-alva):

- *"Create an enrichment profile targeting heads of operations and CFOs."*
- *"Enrich this company looking for its marketing decision-makers."*

Alva never switches on Tier 2 — or mobile numbers in particular — unless you explicitly ask for deeper contact sourcing, so you stay in control of spend.

## Costs

Base enrichment is charged per company when contacts are found. Tier 2 adds a per-contact cost only for the emails or mobiles it actually reveals — you never pay for empty results. See **Settings → Usage** for a breakdown and [Understanding credits](/billing/understanding-credits) for how billing works.
