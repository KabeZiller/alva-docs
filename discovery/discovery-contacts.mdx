title: "Discovery Contacts"
description: "The people your searches and signals have captured, and which of them are already in your CRM."
---

**Discovery → Contacts** lists the people your searches have captured — for example everyone a [People Search](/discovery/people-search) has found, and people named in the news a [Signal](/discovery/signals) picked up. Use it to see who's been found, how to reach them, and whether they're in your CRM yet.

## Opening the page

Go to **Discovery → Contacts** in the sidebar. To see one search's people, click **View Results** on a People Search in your Search Agents list.

## The stats

Across the top you'll see:

| Stat | What it counts |
|------|----------------|
| **Total Contacts** | Everyone captured by your searches |
| **With Email** | Contacts with at least one email address |
| **With Phone** | Contacts with at least one phone number |
| **Imported to CRM** | Contacts that are already CRM contacts |

## What each contact shows

- **Name**, with an **Imported** badge if the person is already in your CRM
- **Seniority** and **job title**
- **Company** they work for
- **Search Agent** that found them
- **Email**, **phone**, or a **LinkedIn** link, where known

Use the search box to find people by name, job title, or company, and sort by name, job title, or date added. People whose company you've discarded aren't listed.

## How contacts reach your CRM

People Search imports the people it finds automatically, so its contacts show as **Imported** straight away. Other captured contacts come into your CRM with their company: when you import a company from the **Found** stage of the [Discovery Pipeline](/discovery/discovery-companies), its captured contacts are imported with it.

## Where enrichment contacts go

The contacts [enrichment](/discovery/enrichment) finds at an imported company go **straight into your CRM** as contacts on that company — they don't wait here. You'll find them on the company's page, ranked by seniority with economic buyers highlighted, and in your main **Contacts** list.

<Note>
  A [BDR Agent](/bdr-agents/overview) works from the contacts on each company's CRM record. To see an agent's emails to a person, open the person or their company and look at its activity.
</Note>
