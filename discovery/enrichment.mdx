title: "Enrichment"
description: "How Alva finds contacts, emails, and key information for the companies you import."
---

When a company is imported into your CRM from Discovery, Alva automatically **enriches** it — finding the right people, their email addresses, and getting the company ready for outreach. It happens in the background, so you don't have to do anything.

Base enrichment runs on **every plan** and is paid for in credits.

## What enrichment does

For each imported company, enrichment:

1. **Cleans up the company name** — so it reads naturally in outreach
2. **Searches for contacts** — people who work at the company, with their job titles and email addresses
3. **Removes bad data** — placeholder names, duplicates, and records that aren't real people
4. **Ranks contacts by seniority** — so the most valuable contacts come first
5. **Flags economic buyers** — contacts whose title matches the economic buyer you've set
6. **Scores fit** — if the search has an Ideal Company Profile, the company gets a match score

## When enrichment runs

Enrichment starts automatically, usually within seconds, when:

- You import a company from the **Found** stage of the [Discovery Pipeline](/discovery/discovery-companies) — one at a time, selected, or in bulk by score
- Auto-import adds a company that meets the match threshold
- A [People Search](/discovery/people-search) or a [Signal](/discovery/signals) imports a company

You can also start it yourself — see [Running enrichment again](#running-enrichment-again).

<Note>
  Enrichment needs credit in your account. If your balance gets very low, new enrichment waits at **Enrichment queued** and carries on by itself once you've topped up.
</Note>

## Controlling who enrichment targets

You decide **who** enrichment looks for. Every [Search Agent](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile) has a **People** section where you set:

| Setting | What it controls |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Enable Enrichment** | Whether contacts are found automatically for this search's companies |
| **Primary Job Functions** | The roles to prioritise (e.g. Head of Marketing, VP Sales) |
| **Secondary Job Functions** | Fallback roles if the primary ones aren't found |
| **Economic Buyer** | The ideal decision-maker title (e.g. CEO, CFO) — matching contacts are flagged and prioritised |

For companies you import **yourself** (by CSV or by hand), apply the same targeting with a reusable [Enrichment Profile](/discovery/enrichment-profiles).

<Note>
  Leave the job functions blank and enrichment still finds contacts — it just won't prioritise a particular role.
</Note>

### Tier 2 Contact Search (Pro)

For companies where you want to go deeper, **Tier 2 Contact Search** finds **more** contacts and reveals **more verified emails** — and, if you choose, **mobile numbers** — at an extra per-contact cost. It's off by default and needs the **Pro plan**. See [Tier 2 Contact Search](/discovery/enrichment-profiles#tier-2-contact-search).

## Enrichment status

Each company shows an enrichment badge so you know where it stands. Hover over it for more detail.

| Badge | What it means |
|-------|--------------|
| **Enrichment queued** | Waiting to start — it will begin shortly |
| **Enriching…** | Enrichment is running now |
| **Enriched** | Finished — contacts and emails were found and added |
| **Complete** | Finished — any contacts found are shown on the company |
| **Enrichment failed** | Something went wrong — you can run it again |
| **No website** | The company has no website and no work-email domain, so there was nothing to search. Any fit score is based on the company's industry and keywords |

<Note>
  BDR Agents only work on companies whose enrichment has finished: **Enriched**, **Complete**, or **No website**. A **No website** company usually came from a [People Search](/discovery/people-search), so the person that search found is already its contact. Companies that are queued or still enriching are skipped and checked again later.
</Note>

## What you'll see after enrichment

Once enrichment has finished, the company's page shows:

- **Contacts** — the people found, ranked by seniority, with economic buyers highlighted
- **Email addresses** — on each contact, plus any general inboxes (such as info@ or hello@) on the company record
- **Email pattern** — if one was detected (e.g. firstname.lastname@company.com)
- **Activity** — a timeline entry summarising what enrichment found

All of this is immediately available to [BDR Agents](/bdr-agents/overview) for outreach.

## Economic buyer

If a Search Agent or Enrichment Profile has an **Economic Buyer** set, contacts whose title matches are flagged. This means:

- They're highlighted on the company's contact list
- BDR Agents prioritise them when choosing who to email first
- They're easy to spot when you review contacts yourself

Common economic buyers include CEO, Managing Director, Head of Operations, or CFO — whoever usually makes the buying decision for what you sell.

## Running enrichment again

Enrichment runs once per company. To run it again:

- **On a company's page** — click **Run Enrichment** (never enriched) or **Retry Enrichment** (after **Enrichment failed**, **Complete**, or **No website**). Companies you added yourself need an [Enrichment Profile](/discovery/enrichment-profiles) linked first, so Alva knows who to look for.
- **On the Discovery Pipeline** — tick companies at the Target or Prospect stage and click **Add to Enrichment Queue**.

## Revealing a contact's details (Pro)

On any contact's page, **Reveal details** looks up a verified email and/or mobile number for that one person. It needs the **Pro plan**, and you're only charged for details that are found.

## Costs

Base enrichment is charged per company when contacts are found, and covers the contact search plus the clean-up and ranking. Tier 2, mobiles, and contact reveals cost extra and are only charged for what's returned. See **Settings → Usage** for a breakdown and [Understanding credits](/billing/understanding-credits) for how billing works.
