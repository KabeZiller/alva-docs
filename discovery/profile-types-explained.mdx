Every Search Agent has a profile type. It describes your **relationship** with the companies the agent finds, and decides which pipeline they land in, which lifecycle stages they follow, and which module of your CRM they appear in.

<Note>
  Profile type is about the relationship, not *how* you search. You choose one for every kind of Search Agent — [Local & service businesses](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile), [Funded & high-growth companies](/discovery/company-intelligence), and [People Search](/discovery/people-search).
</Note>

## The five profile types

### ICP — Ideal Customer Profile

**Purpose:** Find companies that would be ideal customers for your products or services.

**Example description:** "Marketing agencies serving B2B technology companies"

**When companies are imported:**
- Relationship type is set to **Customer**
- Lifecycle stages: Target → Prospect → Lead → Customer
- Companies appear in the **CRM** module

This is the most common profile type and the starting point for most sales teams.

---

### IVP — Ideal Vendor Profile

**Purpose:** Find companies that could sell, distribute, or resell your products and services.

**Example description:** "IT resellers and managed service providers serving small businesses"

**When companies are imported:**
- Relationship type is set to **Vendor**
- Lifecycle stages: Target → Evaluating → Negotiating → Vendor
- Companies appear in the **VRM** (Vendor Relationship Management) module

---

### IPP — Ideal Partner Profile

**Purpose:** Find strategic or channel partners.

**Example description:** "Digital agencies offering web design and development services to small businesses"

**When companies are imported:**
- Relationship type is set to **Partner**
- Lifecycle stages: Target → Engaging → Negotiating → Partner
- Companies appear in the **PRM** (Partner Relationship Management) module

Useful for referral networks, reseller channels, or co-marketing partnerships.

---

### ISP — Ideal Supplier Profile

**Purpose:** Find companies that could supply products or services to your business.

**Example description:** "Plumbing and heating contractors serving residential customers"

**When companies are imported:**
- Relationship type is set to **Supplier**
- Lifecycle stages: Target → Evaluating → Negotiating → Supplier
- Companies appear in the **SRM** (Supplier Relationship Management) module

Use this to build a supply chain, a network of subcontractors, or a pool of service providers.

---

### CP — Competitor Profile

**Purpose:** Find companies that compete with you.

**Example description:** "Sales and CRM software companies serving small and mid-sized businesses"

**When companies are found:**
- Relationship type is set to **Competitor**
- Companies are **added to your competitors** in **Context → Competitors**
- Alva uses them for competitive intelligence and in deal analysis

Use this for competitive research. Competitors don't have a pipeline in the [Discovery Pipeline](/discovery/discovery-companies), and BDR Agents can't target them.

---

## How profile type affects the workflow

| Feature | ICP | IVP | IPP | ISP | CP |
|---------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| CRM module | CRM | VRM | PRM | SRM | — |
| Discovery Pipeline tab | Customers | Vendors | Partners | Suppliers | — |
| BDR Agents can target it | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Available for Signals | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Also added to | — | — | — | — | Context → Competitors |

<Note>
  When you create a BDR Agent, Competitor Profiles aren't offered as targets — you wouldn't normally send automated outreach to competitors.
</Note>

## Signals use the same relationships

[Signals](/discovery/signals) (closed beta, Pro) find companies because something has just happened to them, such as a funding round. When you create a signal, you choose what the companies would be to you — customer, partner, vendor, or supplier — and the companies it adds follow the same stages and land in the same pipeline as a Search Agent of that type.

## Choosing the right profile type

Ask yourself: **what is my relationship with these companies?**

- If you want to **sell to them** → ICP
- If you want them to **sell your products** → IVP
- If you want to **partner with them** → IPP
- If you want them to **supply you** → ISP
- If they **compete with you** → CP

You can run several Search Agents of different types at once. A typical set-up might be one ICP for finding customers, one CP for tracking competitors, and one IPP for building a partner channel.
