The **Lead Sheet** is where outreach turns into real sales conversations. It lists every lead in one place and sorts them by urgency, so you always know what to act on first.

## Opening the Lead Sheet

Go to **Outreach → Lead Sheet** in the sidebar.

## What appears here

- **Companies at the Lead stage** (or **Negotiating** for vendors, partners, and suppliers) — whether a team member moved them there or a prospect replied to a [BDR Agent](/bdr-agents/overview)
- **Companies a BDR Agent is talking to** — engaged, escalated, or taken over by you
- **Landing page form fills** — marked **Lead form**. See [Landing pages](/advertising/landing-pages)
- **LinkedIn Lead Gen form fills** — marked **LinkedIn**, with the campaign name. Test submissions from LinkedIn are labelled **Test** and noted as not a real enquiry. See [Leads and results](/advertising/leads-and-results)

A form fill from someone who gave a company is listed under that company. Someone who didn't name a company is listed as themselves.

Each company appears **once**, even if more than one BDR Agent has worked it — the sheet shows the outreach that most needs a human.

## The stats

| Stat | What it counts |
|------|----------------|
| **Total Leads** | Everything on the sheet |
| **Action Required** | A draft is ready or the agent has escalated |
| **Agent Stuck** | The agent has run out of actions it can take |
| **Gone Cold** | No activity in 14 or more days |
| **Awaiting Reply** | Outreach sent, waiting for the prospect |
| **Human Handling** | You've taken over — you're driving these |

## Priorities

Each lead gets a priority, and the sheet is sorted with the most urgent first:

| Priority | What it means | What to do |
|----------|---------------|------------|
| **Draft Ready** | The agent has drafted a reply for you to check | Review and send, edit, or discard it |
| **Escalated** | The agent has handed the lead to you — usually genuine interest or a question that needs judgement | Read the reason and reply |
| **Agent Stuck** | The agent has nothing left it can do | Decide the next step yourself, or return it to outreach |
| **Gone Cold** | Nothing has happened for 14+ days | Follow up, or put it on hold |
| **Human Handling** | You've taken over the conversation | Keep it moving |
| **Awaiting Reply** | Outreach sent, no response yet | Nothing yet |
| **Active** | Healthy, normal activity | Nothing yet |

Use the tabs above the table to show **All Leads** or one priority at a time.

## Finding leads

- **Search** — matches names, domains, industries, locations, agents, owners, and **custom field values**
- **Custom field filter** — pick a [custom field](/getting-started/account-setup), such as the SDR who calls the contact, and a value
- **Owner filter** — **All owners**, **Unassigned**, or one team member

You can also sort by name, priority, last activity, agent, industry, or owner.

## Each lead

Each row shows the lead's name, relationship, owner, agent, last activity, and the next action. A lead found by a [Signal](/discovery/signals) also shows a **Why now** badge with the event that made it worth contacting — hover over it for the date and summary.

You can assign an **owner** straight from the row, and add **Next action notes** for your team. A note you write replaces the agent's own reasoning on that row.

## Row actions

- **Take over** — step into the conversation yourself. The agent stops drafting and escalating on that thread and the lead shows as **Human Handling**. Replying to the prospect from your own mailbox does this automatically.
- **Hand back to agent** — let the agent carry on.
- **Return to Outreach** — take a company off the Lead Sheet and reset it so the BDR Agent can carry on outreach. Any pending drafts are discarded; its history and contacts are kept.
- **Mark Not Interested…** — put the company on hold for **3 months**, **6 months (recommended)**, **12 months**, or a custom period, with an optional reason. When the hold ends, the company returns to outreach with a fresh approach.

For more on drafts, escalations, and replying as the agent or as yourself, see [Managing replies](/bdr-agents/managing-replies).

## Ask Alva what needs your attention

Alva's Lead Desk works from the same data. Ask *"What needs my attention?"* and Alva gives you the headline numbers, then the most urgent leads with one line each on what to do. You can also ask it to filter by a custom field, assign an owner, or bring a held company back.

<Tip>
  Check the Lead Sheet every day. Drafts and escalations are time-sensitive — the faster you respond, the more leads turn into deals.
</Tip>
