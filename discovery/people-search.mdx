title: "People Search"

**People Search** is one of the three ways a [Search Agent](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile) can search. The two company searches find **companies** first, then look for contacts inside them. People Search works the other way round: you tell Alva the roles you sell to — "Chief Information Security Officer", "Head of Operations", "VP of Finance" — and it finds people in those roles, reveals their verified emails, and imports them into your CRM ready for outreach.

Use People Search when the **job title matters more than the company**.

<Note>
  People Search only finds companies established enough to have that role on record, so it isn't the tool for very small or local businesses — use [Local & service businesses](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile) for those.
</Note>

## People search vs company search

| | Company search | People search |
|---|----------------|---------------|
| **You describe** | The kind of company (sector, services, market) | The job titles you want to reach |
| **Alva finds** | Companies, then contacts within them | People in that role, at any company |
| **Best for** | Targeting a market or industry | Targeting a role wherever it sits |
| **Result** | Companies scored by fit, contacts found by enrichment | One person per company with a verified email, imported directly |

For the company searches, see [Local & service businesses](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile) and [Funded & high-growth companies](/discovery/company-intelligence).

## Creating a people search

Go to **Discovery → Search Agents**, click **Create Search Agent**, then choose **Find people**. Pick the **Pipeline** (profile type) the people should land in and give the search a name.

### Job titles (required)

Enter one or more job titles — for example `CISO`, `Chief Information Security Officer`, `Head of Security`. Adding more titles broadens the search.

**Include similar titles** is on by default, so "CISO" also matches "Head of Security". Untick it when you need an exact match.

### Optional filters

| Filter | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Seniority** | Restrict to seniority levels (e.g. C-suite, VP, Director, Manager) |
| **Person Location** | Where the person is based — country, region, or city |
| **Company HQ Location** | Where the person's company is headquartered |
| **Company Size (employees)** | Employee-count bands |
| **Industry / Keywords** | Industry or product-category tags, such as technology, SaaS, or fintech. A company only needs to match **one** tag, so each extra tag broadens the search |
| **Free-text Keywords** | A word or two the company should be associated with, such as "stadium". **Every word must match**, so keep this to one or two terms — a longer list often returns nothing |

<Tip>
  Commas in **Free-text Keywords** don't mean "any of these". If you have a list of alternative industries, put them in **Industry / Keywords** instead. Put go-to-market fit, such as B2B or enterprise, in the Ideal Company Profile rather than in either keyword filter.
</Tip>

Fewer filters return more people. Start broad and narrow later if you're getting low-quality matches.

### Result limit

Every people search has a result limit — **Stop after this many companies** (default **25**). The search finds **one person per company** and **pauses automatically** when it has imported that many companies, so a large organisation with many matching people can't use up your limit on its own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start small">
    Set a low limit — say 10 — when you first activate the search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the results">
    Check the people that were imported. Are they the right roles? At the right kind of company?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise the limit">
    Happy with the quality? Increase the limit on the Search Agent's page. The search **resumes automatically** and runs until it reaches the new limit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  People searches have no throttle speed. The result limit sets the pace.
</Note>

### Ideal Company Profile (optional)

Add an optional **Ideal Company Profile** — a short, plain-English description of the kind of company you'd ideally reach these people at. Alva uses it to **score** each person's company for fit after import, so your best-fit contacts can be prioritised.

This is scoring, not filtering: a low score never stops a contact being imported. The brief follows the same rules as a company search description — it can only ask for things a company's website shows, so avoid headcount, funding, or technology. See [what makes a good description](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile#step-2-describe-what-youre-looking-for).

### Reveal mobile numbers (optional, Pro)

People Search reveals verified **emails**. If you also want direct mobile numbers, tick **Also reveal mobile numbers** when you create the search, or **Reveal mobile numbers** on the Search Agent's page later.

- It's **off by default** and needs the **Pro plan**.
- You're charged per mobile, **only when a number is actually delivered**. Numbers arrive a few minutes after the person is imported.
- Turning it on for an existing search also requests mobiles for the people it has **already imported**.

## What gets imported

When the search runs, Alva reveals the verified email for each person it picks and imports them into your CRM automatically:

- **Contacts** are created with their name, job title, verified email, and company.
- **Companies** are created, or matched to an existing record by domain so you never get duplicates. They land in your [Discovery Pipeline](/discovery/discovery-companies) at the **Target** stage.
- Each company goes through standard [enrichment](/discovery/enrichment), which adds a website summary, finds more contacts, and scores fit if you set a brief.
- The people appear in [Discovery → Contacts](/discovery/discovery-contacts). Click **View Results** on the Search Agent to see just this search's people.

Only people with a discoverable email are revealed and imported, so you never pay for empty records. Some emails come back **verified** and others **unverified**. Both are imported, but [BDR Agents](/bdr-agents/overview) only send automatically to high-confidence addresses — unverified ones are kept for you to use manually.

### Companies with no website

Sometimes a person's company has no website on record. Alva tries the domain of the person's work email first. If there's still no website, the company shows a **No website** badge instead of an enrichment status: there's no domain to search for more contacts, so the person you found is the contact. Its fit score is worked out from the company's industry and keywords instead of its website.

## Match scoring

If you set an Ideal Company Profile, each person's company is scored **0–100** for fit shortly after import:

| Result | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Match** | At or above the search's match threshold — a strong fit with your brief |
| **Worth a look** | A little below the threshold — a partial fit |
| **Not a match** | Well below the threshold — a weak fit |

The score appears as a **% match** badge and a Profile Match Analysis on the company's detail page.

- **Nothing is ever discarded.** A low score never holds a contact back — the score only sets priority.
- **BDR Agents work best-fit first.** You can also set a [minimum match score](/bdr-agents/setting-up-your-first-agent) on an agent so it skips low-fit companies.
- **No brief means no score.** Without an Ideal Company Profile, companies are left unscored rather than assumed to be a perfect match.

## Editing a search

You can edit a people search's criteria while it's in **draft** or **paused** — pause an active search first. Saving returns the search to draft so reactivating runs cleanly against the new criteria. People already imported stay in your CRM. To find **more** with the new criteria, raise the result limit above the current count and reactivate.

## How duplicates are avoided

- **Before revealing an email**, people Alva has already returned to you are skipped — no repeat charge.
- **On import**, anyone whose email is already a CRM contact is skipped.
- **Companies** are matched to existing records by domain, so each organisation is a single CRM company.

## Managing People Search with Alva

Ask [Alva](/ai-assistant/talking-to-alva):

- *"Find me CISOs at UK fintechs and get their emails."*
- *"Set up a people search for Heads of Operations at logistics companies in Germany, limit 20."*
- *"Pause my security officers search."*
- *"Raise the limit on my CISO search to 50."*

If you ask for several kinds of buyer at once — say, finance directors at manufacturers *and* operations heads at retailers — Alva creates **one search per kind of buyer**, so each search's titles make sense for its industries. It also keeps CEO and Managing Director titles to smaller organisations. When a request could mean a company search or a people search, Alva asks which you want.

## Costs

- **Searching is free.**
- **You pay per verified email revealed** — one person per company, and only for people who actually have an email.
- **Mobiles** (Pro, optional) are charged only when a number arrives.
- **No repeat charges** for people you've already imported.

See **Settings → Usage** for a breakdown and [Understanding credits](/billing/understanding-credits) for how billing works.
