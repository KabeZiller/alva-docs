---
title: "Scheduled Report Delivery"
description: "Have a custom report run and emailed to you or your team automatically, as a CSV attachment."
---

You can schedule any [custom report](/reporting/building-a-custom-report) to run automatically and arrive by email. It's an easy way to get a weekly pipeline list or a monthly new-contacts list without having to remember to check.

## Setting up a schedule

Open the report from the **Reports** page. Beside the results you'll see the **Schedule Report** card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch on scheduling">
    Turn on the switch at the top of the **Schedule Report** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it runs">
    Set the options below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add recipients">
    Type an email address under **Email Recipients** and press **Enter** or click **+**. Add as many addresses as you like — they don't need to be Alva users. Click the **×** on an address to remove it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Schedule**. You need at least one recipient to save a schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Frequency** | **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly** |
| **Day of Week** | For weekly reports — which day to send |
| **Day of Month** | For monthly reports — which date to send |
| **Time (24-hour format)** | The time the report runs, for example `09:00` |

Once saved, the card shows the **Next scheduled run**.

## What gets delivered

Each scheduled email is titled **Scheduled Report:** followed by the report name, and carries the report's results as a **CSV attachment** you can open in Excel or Google Sheets. The report runs fresh each time, so the attachment always reflects your latest data.

<Note>
  If a report has no matching records when it runs, no email is sent.
</Note>

## Changing or stopping a schedule

Open the report and update the **Schedule Report** card — change the frequency, time, or recipients — then click **Save Schedule**. To stop the emails, turn off the switch and click **Save Schedule**.

Deleting a report also deletes its schedule.

## Using Alva

You can ask [Alva](/ai-assistant/talking-to-alva) to manage schedules in chat — for example, "Email the open deals report to me and Sam every Monday at 8am." Alva can set up, change, or pause a report's schedule, and can email a report as a CSV straight away if you need it now.

<Tip>
  A weekly pipeline report delivered every Monday morning is a great way to start the week with a clear picture of your business. For outreach figures, Alva's [weekly business development report](/reporting/outreach-reporting#the-weekly-business-development-report) does the same job.
</Tip>
