The Analytics page gives you a visual overview of your pipeline health, your win rate, how well deals follow your sales methodology, and the latest AI-generated insights.

Click **Analytics** in the sidebar to open it.

## Headline figures

Four cards across the top give you the numbers at a glance:

| Card | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Total Pipeline** | The combined value of your open deals, and how many active deals there are. Won and lost deals aren't included |
| **Win Rate** | The share of your closed deals that you won, with the number won and lost |
| **Methodology Adherence** | How completely your active deals' [sales methodology](/crm/sales-methodologies) fields are filled in, on average |
| **AI Insights** | How many recent AI findings are in the panel below (up to five) |

## Charts

- **Pipeline Overview** — a bar chart of deal value across your pipeline stages
- **Win/Loss Ratio** — your win rate, with won and lost deals side by side
- **Methodology Adherence** — average completion for each methodology you use, with the number of deals behind each figure

## AI insights

The **AI Insights** panel lists Alva's five most recent automated findings about your deals — risks, opportunities, and health scores — each with the date it was found and, where relevant, a risk level of high, medium, or low.

Alva generates these insights in the background on a regular schedule, so the panel refreshes on its own. On a new account, the panel stays empty until the first findings have been generated.
