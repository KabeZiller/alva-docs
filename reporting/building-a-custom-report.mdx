---
title: "Building a Custom Report"
description: "Build a saved table of your deals, companies, or contacts, then run it, export it, or email it on a schedule."
---

A custom report is a saved table of your CRM data. You choose what to report on, which columns to show, and which records to include — then run it whenever you need the latest figures, download it as a CSV, or have it [emailed on a schedule](/reporting/scheduled-report-delivery).

## Creating a report

Click **Reports** in the sidebar, then **New Report**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name your report">
    Under **Basic Information**, enter a **Report Name** and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a data source">
    Pick the **Data Source**: **Deals**, **Companies**, or **Contacts**. Changing the data source resets your columns and filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your columns">
    Under **Columns**, tick the fields you want in the table. **Name** and **Created At** are ticked for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add filters (optional)">
    Under **Filters**, click **Add Filter** to narrow down the records. Each filter has a **Field**, an **Operator**, and a **Value**. Add as many filters as you like — a record must match all of them to appear. With no filters, every record is included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the report">
    Click **Create Report**. Alva saves it and opens the results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Available columns

| Data source | Columns |
|-------------|---------|
| **Deals** | Name, Value, Stage, Probability, Expected Close Date, Methodology, Created At |
| **Companies** | Name, Domain, Industry, Size, Location, Website, Created At |
| **Contacts** | First Name, Last Name, Email, Title, Phone, Created At |

### Filter operators

| Operator | Matches records where the field… |
|----------|----------------------------------|
| **Equals** | Is exactly the value |
| **Contains** | Includes the value anywhere in the text |
| **Greater Than** | Is above the value |
| **Less Than** | Is below the value |

<Tip>
  **Contains** matches partial text and ignores capitals, so it's the most forgiving choice for text fields. For example, to find companies in a city or region, add a filter on **Location** with **Contains**.
</Tip>

## Viewing and running a report

The **Reports** page lists your saved reports with their name, description, created date, and type. Click **View Report** to run one — the report runs against your latest data every time you open it.

The results table shows your chosen columns and how many records matched. A report shows up to 1,000 records.

## Exporting to CSV

On the report, click **Export CSV** to download the results as a spreadsheet you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.

## Scheduling a report

To have a report emailed to you or your team automatically, use the **Schedule Report** card beside the results. See [Scheduled report delivery](/reporting/scheduled-report-delivery).

## Changing or deleting a report

You can't edit a saved report's columns or filters. To change what a report shows, create a new one and delete the old one.

To delete a report, click the delete button on its card on the **Reports** page. Deleting a report also stops any schedule it has.

## Using Alva

[Alva](/ai-assistant/talking-to-alva) can work with your saved reports in chat. Alva can:

- List your reports and their schedules
- Run a report and show you the results
- Create a new report — Alva confirms the data source, columns, and filters with you before saving
- Set up, change, or pause a report's email schedule
- Email a report as a CSV straight away
- Delete a report, after confirming with you

For a one-off question you don't need to save, just ask — "Which deals over £50k are expected to close this quarter?" — and Alva looks it up directly.
