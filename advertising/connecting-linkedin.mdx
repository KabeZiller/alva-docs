---
title: "Connecting LinkedIn"
description: "Connect your LinkedIn ad account to Alva, choose the account Alva uses, and keep the connection healthy."
---

An admin connects LinkedIn once, at **Settings → Advertising**. After that, anyone on your team can ask Alva to draft and run campaigns on the ad account you choose.

## Before you start

You need these in place on LinkedIn first. Alva can't create them for you.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A LinkedIn Company Page you administer">
    Your ads run under your Company Page's name and logo. The person who connects must be an admin of that Page, and must stay one: if they lose Page admin, the connection stops working and someone needs to reconnect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A LinkedIn ad account">
    Create one in LinkedIn Campaign Manager if you don't have one yet. Alva works with one ad account at a time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A payment method on the ad account">
    LinkedIn bills your ad account directly. You add the card in Campaign Manager. Alva never holds your media budget.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A role that can manage campaigns">
    The person connecting needs a role on the ad account such as account manager, campaign manager, creative manager or billing admin. Accounts where they have no suitable role are shown but can't be selected.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Connect your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the advertising settings">
    Go to **Settings → Advertising**. Only admins see the **Connect LinkedIn** button; other team members see a note that an admin needs to do this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to LinkedIn">
    Click **Connect LinkedIn**, sign in with the LinkedIn account that administers your Company Page, and approve access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the ad account">
    Back in Alva, the **Ad accounts** list shows every account LinkedIn returned, with its company, your role, currency and status. Click **Use this account** on the one Alva should use. It's then marked **Selected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reading the ad account status

| Status | What it means |
|--|--|
| **Active** | Ready. Campaigns can be drafted and go live. |
| **On hold** | Usually a missing payment method. Add one in Campaign Manager, then click **Refresh**. |
| **Draft** | The account set-up isn't finished in Campaign Manager. |
| **Cancelled** | The account is closed on LinkedIn. |

<Note>
  You can draft and preview campaigns while LinkedIn has your account on a billing hold. Alva won't switch a campaign on until the hold clears.
</Note>

If you add a card, create a new ad account or get a new role in Campaign Manager, click **Refresh** on the **Ad accounts** card (or ask Alva to refresh). You don't need to reconnect.

## Lead form fills

When the connection is set up for leads, **Settings → Advertising** says that lead form fills from this connection sync into Alva. If it asks you to reconnect LinkedIn to start receiving lead form fills, click **Reconnect LinkedIn** once. Until you do, form fills stay in LinkedIn.

## Keeping the connection healthy

LinkedIn access expires from time to time. If the connection is about to expire, or Alva can't renew it, the connection is marked as being in error and the admin who connected it gets one email. Reconnect at **Settings → Advertising** to fix it.

## Disconnecting

An admin can click **Disconnect** at **Settings → Advertising**. Draft campaigns stay in Alva. You can connect again at any time with **Connect LinkedIn**.

## What Alva can and can't do here

Alva can tell you whether LinkedIn is connected, list the ad accounts it can see with their role and status, explain a hold, and refresh the account list. Connecting, disconnecting and choosing the ad account happen on the settings page, so Alva takes you there.

## Ask Alva

- "Is LinkedIn connected?"
- "Which ad account are we using, and is it ready to go live?"
- "I've just added a card in Campaign Manager. Can you refresh the ad accounts?"
- "Why is our ad account on hold?"
