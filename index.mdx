## What Alva does

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="CRM">
    Track your companies, contacts, deals, and contracts in one place. Alva scores your deals, flags risks, and tells you what needs attention without you having to ask. See [Companies and contacts](/crm/companies-and-contacts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI assistant">
    Ask Alva anything about your business in plain language, in the chat panel or by emailing your account's own Alva address. She reads your deals, emails, and activity, and can carry out the work for you — always asking for your yes before anything that spends money or contacts people. See [Talking to Alva](/ai-assistant/talking-to-alva) and [Alva by Email](/ai-assistant/alva-by-email).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Alva's agenda and weekly report">
    Alva keeps her own agenda of reminders and reviews, follows up on what you ask her to check, and can send you a weekly business development report with recommendations you can answer with a simple "yes". See [Agenda and weekly report](/ai-assistant/agenda-and-weekly-report).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Discovery">
    Find companies that match your ideal customer profile across more than 4.4 million locations worldwide, or search for the people who make the buying decision. Alva enriches each company with contacts and checks how well it fits. See [Creating a Search Agent](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile) and [People search](/discovery/people-search).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Signals (closed beta, Pro)">
    Describe an event in plain English — a funding round, a new hire, an upcoming project — and Alva looks for companies where it has just happened, so you can reach out at the right moment. See [Signals](/discovery/signals).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pulse (Pro)">
    Keep up with your sector. Pulse watches the news topics and websites you choose, turns what's new into a feed with suggested angles, and emails you a digest. See [Pulse](/discovery/pulse).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI BDR agents">
    Agents that choose who to contact, write personalised emails, follow up, and hand genuine replies to you — within the budget and hours you set. See [How BDR agents work](/bdr-agents/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="LinkedIn ads and landing pages">
    Connect your own LinkedIn ad account and let Alva draft, preview, and run Lead Gen campaigns, with ad images and landing pages built from your brand. See [Advertising overview](/advertising/overview) and [Landing pages](/advertising/landing-pages).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Calling">
    Call prospects from your browser, with optional recording and transcripts, and outcomes logged against the right records. Calling sessions for working through a call list need Pro. See [Making calls](/calling/making-calls).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Email intelligence">
    Connect your inbox and Alva reads every conversation. She picks out commitments, sentiment, and next steps, and keeps your CRM up to date without manual data entry. See [Understanding email analysis](/email/understanding-email-analysis).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Documents">
    Ask Alva for a plan, a one-pager, a briefing, or an outreach report as a PDF. Every document she writes is kept on the **Documents** page. See [Documents](/ai-assistant/documents).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reporting">
    Track pipeline performance, outreach, and revenue trends with built-in reports, or build your own and have them emailed to you on a schedule. See [Outreach reporting](/reporting/outreach-reporting).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How you pay

Alva is pay as you go. Every new account starts with a starter credit, with no card needed. When you want to keep going, you add a card, set a monthly cap, and switch on auto top-up. The Pro plan gives you a better rate and unlocks Pro features. See [Understanding credits](/billing/understanding-credits) and [Plans and pricing](/billing/plans-and-pricing).
