---
title: "Configuring Budgets and Hours"
description: "Control how much your agent spends, when it works, how fast it goes and how it follows up."
---

BDR agents run autonomously, so it's important to set clear boundaries on spending, scheduling and pace. You configure these when creating the agent and can change them at any time with **Edit Configuration**.

## Monthly budget

Every agent has a **Monthly Budget Limit**, set with a slider on the **Budget Configuration** card. It's the most the agent can spend in a calendar month across all companies.

When the agent has used its monthly budget, it **stays Active but skips its work** until the budget resets. Nothing is sent and nothing more is spent. The budget resets automatically at the start of each calendar month. To carry on sooner, raise the limit, or choose **Reset Monthly Budget** from the agent's **⋯** menu to set this month's spend back to zero.

The agent also needs credits on your account to run. See [Understanding credits](/billing/understanding-credits).

### What counts towards the budget

The agent's actions themselves (sending emails, creating contacts, updating statuses, escalating and drafting replies) are **free**.

The budget covers the AI processing behind the scenes when the agent evaluates each company and decides what to do. Finding contacts and emails is billed separately as part of [enrichment](/discovery/enrichment).

<Note>
  **Personalised agents spend more per company than Template agents.** They do extra work (refreshing [prospect intel](/bdr-agents/personalised-outreach#prospect-intel-refresh), composing original copy and running extra checks), so budget accordingly. Most of the heavier spend is during [setup and testing](/bdr-agents/personalised-outreach#a-note-on-cost).
</Note>

### Per-company budget tiers

**Per-Company Budget Tiers** limit how much the agent spends on any one company, based on its match score. Higher-scoring companies get a bigger allowance; borderline matches get less. By default there are four tiers: 90–100%, 80–89%, 70–79% and below 70%. You can edit the amounts, change the ranges, and add or remove tiers.

The **Cost by Company** card on the agent's page shows where the money has gone.

## Operating hours

Switch on **Operating Hours** to restrict when the agent works, so outreach lands during business hours. When it's off, the agent works around the clock.

- **Timezone**: the timezone for the schedule (default London)
- **Use company HQ timezone**: apply your hours in each company's own headquarters timezone instead (see below)
- **Start Time** and **End Time**: for example 09:00 to 17:00
- **Active Days**: which days of the week the agent may work (default Monday to Friday). **Weekdays only** and **Every day** are quick presets.

Outside its hours, the agent waits for the next window. No work is lost; it picks up where it left off. Replies from prospects are always handled straight away, and **Run Now** isn't restricted by operating hours.

### Use company HQ timezone

Your prospects may be spread across the world. Switch on **Use company HQ timezone** and the agent applies your start time, end time and days in **each company's** local time, worked out from the company's headquarters location. A company in New York is emailed during New York business hours and a company in London during London business hours, from the same agent.

When this is on, the **Timezone** field becomes **Fallback timezone**: it's used for any company whose HQ location can't be worked out.

<Tip>
  With a single timezone, the whole agent pauses outside the window. With company HQ timezones, the agent keeps running and simply holds each company until it's inside its own local hours.
</Tip>

## Run pace

**Run Pace** sets how many companies the agent evaluates per **10-minute cycle**:

- **Range:** 1 to 100 companies per cycle
- **Default:** 1 company per cycle, the slowest pace, ideal for reviewing each decision
- **2–10:** a careful pace for monitoring a new agent
- **11–50:** a normal pace for an agent you've checked
- **Over 50:** only when you're confident in the agent's behaviour

A higher pace means faster outreach, but the agent is always capped by its monthly budget and by how many emails its sending accounts can send each day.

You can also choose **Run Now (5 companies)** from the agent's **⋯** menu to process a batch straight away.

## Follow-up cadence

The **Follow-up Cadence** card has its own switch. With follow-ups off, the agent sends only the first email to each contact.

| Setting | Default | What it controls |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| **Minimum days before follow-up** | 3 days | How long to wait after an unanswered email before sending the next |
| **Maximum follow-ups per contact** | 2 | How many unanswered follow-ups before the agent moves on to the next contact at the company |
| **Follow-up Template Groups** | None (optional) | Templates to use for follow-ups |

If you don't choose any follow-up templates, the agent **writes its own brief follow-ups** (up to 75 words), on the same topic as the first email.

<Note>
  The cadence applies per contact, not per company. If the agent works several contacts at the same company, each gets their own sequence. Follow-ups reply in the same email thread as the original.
</Note>

<Note>
  In **Personalised** mode, follow-ups can introduce **new offers and angles** from your [playbook](/bdr-agents/personalised-outreach#multi-offer-follow-ups) so a sequence stays fresh rather than repeating the same ask.
</Note>

## Sending limits

How many emails an agent can send in a day also depends on its sending accounts:

- **Each mailbox has a daily sending limit.** The agent rotates across its sending accounts. When every one has reached its limit, the agent shows **Daily email quota reached — resets at midnight** and waits.
- **Mailboxes marked Do not send** in [sending health](/email/sending-health-and-limits) are skipped for new outreach. If every sending account is marked, the agent waits until one is fixed. Mailboxes marked **Caution** keep sending.
- **New outreach mailboxes warm up.** [Outreach mailboxes](/email/outreach-mailboxes) Alva sets up for you send no outreach for their first 4 days, and are limited to 5 a day for the first 7 days.
- **New accounts have an account-wide cap** for their first 30 days: 20 a day in the first week, 50 in the second, then 100. All outreach pauses if the bounce rate goes over 8% or three or more people reply asking to be removed.

Replies to prospects are never held back by any of these limits. See [Sending health and limits](/email/sending-health-and-limits) for the full rules.

## Changing settings on a live agent

You can edit any of these settings while the agent is running. Go to **Outreach → BDR Agents**, open the agent, then choose **Edit Configuration** from the **⋯** menu.

Changes take effect from the next cycle. The agent won't interrupt an action already in progress.

You can pause and restart an agent at any time with **Pause Agent** and **Start Agent** on its page.
