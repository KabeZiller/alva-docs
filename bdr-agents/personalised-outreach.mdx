A **Personalised** agent writes its own copy rather than sending fixed templates. It composes each email from a **playbook** you define (your tone, the offers you want to lead with, and the things it must always or never say), grounded in what it knows about the specific prospect.

This makes Personalised agents far more flexible than Template agents, but also more involved to set up correctly.

<Warning>
  **Set up your Personalised agent through [Alva](/ai-assistant/talking-to-alva).** Alva builds the playbook with you, runs sample previews and only lets the agent go live once everything is in place. Building one from scratch in the agent form is **not encouraged**. Once it exists, small tweaks in the form (budget, hours, adjusting an offer) are fine.

  Just open the chat and say, for example: _"Set up a Personalised BDR agent for my healthcare Search Agent."_
</Warning>

## The playbook

The playbook is the heart of a Personalised agent. It lives **on the agent itself** (not in your email templates) and mixes structured guidance with free-form notes, so you get the reliability of clear rules with the flexibility of writing in your own words.

| Part of the playbook | What it's for |
|----------------------|---------------|
| **Tone** | How the copy should sound: for example warm and direct, formal, or peer-to-peer and concise |
| **Offers to drip** | The angles the agent can lead with, in order. Each offer has a name and how it helps the prospect. The agent can drip different offers across a follow-up sequence. |
| **Must include** | Points that should appear in outreach (for example a specific credential or compliance note) |
| **Never claim** | Claims the agent must never make: your guardrail against overreach |
| **CTA style** | How the call to action should feel: for example a soft "worth a quick chat?" or a concrete meeting request |
| **Length hint** | Roughly how long emails should be |
| **Freeform notes** | Anything else in your own words: context, do's and don'ts, phrasing you like |

<Tip>
  You don't have to fill in every field. A playbook is complete when it has at least **one offer** (name and angle) and either a **tone** or some **freeform notes**. Alva will help you flesh out the rest.
</Tip>

### Multi-offer follow-ups

A Personalised agent can **introduce new angles across the sequence**. If the first email led with one offer and got no reply, a follow-up might try a different offer from your playbook rather than repeating the original ask. This keeps a sequence feeling fresh and human instead of nagging.

### UK or US spelling

Personalised emails, previews and draft replies are written in UK or US English based on the prospect company's headquarters. Companies based in the United States get US English; every other country, and companies whose HQ is unknown, get UK English.

## Prospect intel refresh

Personalised agents can **refresh what they know about a prospect before writing**, pulling a fresh summary of the company's website and public company profile so the copy reflects the prospect as they are today.

- The **Refresh prospect intel before compose** switch is **on by default**. You can turn it off.
- On first outreach it gathers fresh context; later it only tops up when the stored summary is thin or missing.
- It never blocks outreach. If a refresh can't complete, the agent writes from whatever it already has.
- The small extra cost is covered by the agent's [budget](/bdr-agents/configuring-budgets-and-hours#monthly-budget).

<Note>
  Template agents don't use prospect intel refresh: they rely on your templates and merge fields.
</Note>

## Signals: why now

If your account has [Signals](/discovery/signals), you can link signal profiles to a Personalised agent. Each company a signal finds carries its event (a funding round, a new hire, an upcoming event) into the agent's writing:

- **The first email leads with the event** when it happened in the last 45 days. It mentions it once, never says how the company was found, never pastes a source link, only congratulates on good news, and never invents amounts, investors or dates.
- **Follow-ups don't repeat it.**
- **Each signal's outreach angle travels with its companies**, so **one Personalised agent can work every signal** you have. You don't need one agent per signal.
- **The agent won't claim a recent event** at the prospect that isn't in its research or the signal. An email that does is blocked before it sends.

When an agent links a signal profile, the companies with the newest events are worked first. See [How the agent chooses who to work on](/bdr-agents/how-the-agent-works#signal-profiles).

## Previewing before you launch

Before a Personalised agent sends anything, ask Alva to **preview** what it would write. Previews are samples only: **nothing is ever sent to a prospect**.

- Alva writes sample emails for a few real companies from your Search Agent (**3 by default**). When the agent links a signal profile, the samples favour companies with a recent event.
- You read the copy exactly as the prospect would receive it, **including the signature** of the agent's first sending account.
- Every sample is **spam-scored** from 0 to 100 (100 is clean), signature included, just like a real send. Alva reports each score and the average, and points out anything in your signature or settings that's dragging the score down.
- Tell Alva what you liked, what you didn't and what to change, then preview again. Each round rotates through different companies.

<Tip>
  Treat previews as a conversation. Loop with Alva ("make it shorter", "lead with the second offer", "drop that claim") until the copy consistently sounds right and scores well. Alva won't declare the agent ready to activate on your behalf; that's your call.
</Tip>

## Going live

A Personalised agent will only start once it passes a **hard checklist**. This protects you from launching an agent that would send weak or blocked outreach. To go live, the agent needs:

<Steps>
  <Step title="At least one sending account">
    Required before the agent can even be saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A linked Search Agent with companies to work on">
    At least one enriched, eligible company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A complete playbook">
    At least one offer, plus a tone or freeform notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A preview that averages a spam score of 70 or more">
    Emails scoring below 70 are blocked before sending, so a preview averaging below 70 means most of the agent's emails wouldn't get out. Tighten the playbook (shorter, plainer, no promotional wording or links) and preview again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If **Escalate to Human** is on, the agent also needs at least one person under **Notify Users**. On newer accounts, starting also needs the Pro plan. Once the checklist passes, Alva (or you, with **Start Agent** on the agent page) can switch the agent to **Active**.

<Note>
  If anything's missing, the agent card shows exactly what's outstanding, and Alva will tell you what's left before it lets the agent go live.
</Note>

## A note on cost

Personalised agents do more thinking than Template agents (researching the prospect, composing original copy and running safety checks), so setting one up and testing it properly uses more credits than a Template agent. Most of that spend is **one-time setup and testing**, not a recurring fee, and it buys you materially stronger outreach. See [Understanding credits](/billing/understanding-credits).

## After launch

Once live, a Personalised agent behaves like any other agent: it works through companies in [new-first order](/bdr-agents/how-the-agent-works#who-the-agent-contacts-first), follows its [budget and hours](/bdr-agents/configuring-budgets-and-hours), spam-scores every email before it sends, escalates genuine replies, and steps aside when you [take over](/bdr-agents/managing-replies#taking-over-a-conversation). You can keep refining the playbook with Alva at any time.
