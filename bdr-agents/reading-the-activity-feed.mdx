title: "Reading the Activity Feed"
description: "Follow what your BDR agent has done, why it's idle, and how its emails are performing."
---

Every BDR agent has a detail page with live stats, email performance and a feed of every action it has taken. It's your window into what the agent is doing and how it's performing, and it works the same for [Template and Personalised](/bdr-agents/overview) agents.

## The agent detail page

Go to **Outreach → BDR Agents** and click an agent's name. At the top of the page you'll find the agent's status, its controls and an **Ops Room** button that opens the live [Ops Room](/bdr-agents/ops-room) in a new tab.

### Stats

Five cards sit at the top of the page:

| Card | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Companies Evaluated** | How many companies the agent has worked on, out of the total in scope |
| **Leads Generated** | Companies escalated to you with genuine interest |
| **Emails Sent** | Total outreach emails sent |
| **Replies Received** | Genuine replies, with the reply rate |
| **Budget (MTD)** | Spend this month, with a bar showing how much of the monthly limit is used |

### Agent status

| Status | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Active** | Working through companies every 10 minutes during its operating hours |
| **Paused** | Not sending new outreach or follow-ups, but [still handling replies](/bdr-agents/managing-replies#paused-agents-still-handle-replies) |
| **Completed** | All target companies have been worked |

### Why an agent is idle

An active agent that isn't doing anything right now shows **Agent is live but currently idle**, with the reason:

| Idle reason | What to do |
|-------------|------------|
| **Outside operating hours — next window: …** | Nothing. The agent picks up when the next window opens. See [Operating hours](/bdr-agents/configuring-budgets-and-hours#operating-hours). |
| **No companies to process right now** | Wait for your Search Agents to find more companies, link another Search Agent, or check your minimum match score. |
| **Daily email quota reached — resets at midnight** | Nothing, or add more sending accounts to the agent. |
| **All sending accounts are marked do-not-send — fix sending health** | Review your mailboxes in **Settings → Email Connections**. See [Sending health and limits](/email/sending-health-and-limits). |

When an agent has used its monthly budget, it stays **Active** but skips its work until the budget resets: check the **Budget (MTD)** card.

### Mailbox banners

The page warns you about the agent's sending accounts:

- **A sending account is marked do not send**: that mailbox is skipped for new outreach until its sending health is fixed. Click **Review**.
- **A sending account needs caution**: Caution doesn't block sending. Click **Review**, or **Remind me in 14 days** to hide the banner.
- **A sending account has no email signature**: outbound emails from that mailbox go without a sign-off. Click **Fix** to add one.

If your account needs the Pro plan to send outreach, a **Sending outreach needs the Pro plan** banner appears too.

## Email performance

Once the agent has sent its first email, the **Email Performance** card shows:

| Metric | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Open Rate** | The share of emails opened. Shows **N/A — not tracked** when the agent's **Track opens** setting is off (the default). |
| **Reply Rate** | The share of emails that got a genuine reply (bounces and out-of-office replies don't count) |
| **Bounce Rate** | The share of emails that bounced permanently. A temporary delivery delay isn't a bounce. |
| **OOO** | How many out-of-office replies came back |

Click any of these to open the **list of emails** behind the figure: recipient, contact or company, subject, when it was sent, and when it was opened, replied to, bounced or got an out-of-office reply. Click an address to go to the contact or company.

Below the metrics you'll see the **average spam score** across the agent's scored emails (100 is clean; emails below 70 are blocked before sending). If the agent writes to generic inboxes such as info@, a comparison shows reply and opt-out rates for generic inboxes against named contacts.

## Configuration and pipeline

The **Configuration** card summarises the agent's instructions, generic-address setting, target Search Agents, allowed tools, template groups, operating hours, open tracking and previously-contacted setting.

The **Outreach Pipeline** card shows how the agent's companies are spread across outreach stages.

## The outreach lifecycle

Each company the agent works on moves through these stages. They drive which companies the agent picks up next and how they appear on the [Lead Sheet](/discovery/lead-sheet).

| Stage | What it means | Still worked by the agent? |
|-------|--------------|----------------------------|
| **Target** | Ready, not yet contacted | Yes |
| **Prospecting** | First email sent, awaiting a reply or follow-up | Yes |
| **Engaged** | The prospect replied and the agent is mid-conversation | Yes |
| **Lead** | Escalated to a person: genuine interest detected | No |
| **Human handling** | You've [taken over](/bdr-agents/managing-replies#taking-over-a-conversation) the conversation | No |
| **Disqualified** | Bounced, opted out or marked Not Interested | No |

## The activity feed

The **Recent Activity** card lists every action, newest first, with **Previous** and **Next** buttons and a choice of how many to **Show** per page. Each entry shows:

- **What the agent did**, for example **Send Email**, **Create Contact**, **Update Status**, **Escalate**, **Draft Reply** or **Email Safety Check**
- **The company**, linked to its record
- **Whether it succeeded**, with details of the result (for a sent email, who it went to and the subject)
- **The agent's reasoning**, in its own words
- **When** it happened

The agent's reasoning explains why it chose this action, why it picked this contact, or why it's escalating. Use it to understand the agent's decisions and adjust your instructions or [playbook](/bdr-agents/personalised-outreach#the-playbook) if needed.

### How the agent reads replies

When a prospect replies, the agent works out what kind of reply it is:

| Reply | What happens |
|-------|--------------|
| **Genuine interest or a question** | The agent escalates, and drafts a reply if **Draft Replies** is on |
| **Not interested** | Escalated so you can decide; you can put the company on a [timed hold](/bdr-agents/managing-replies#not-interested-try-again-later) |
| **Out of office** | Counted under **OOO**; the agent waits and tries again later |
| **Bounce** | The address is treated as undeliverable and won't be emailed again |

## Blocked emails

If the [spam-score check](/bdr-agents/how-the-agent-works#the-spam-score-check) or another safety check blocks an email, the feed shows an **Email Safety Check** entry with the score, the subject, a preview, and the issues that stopped it. Click **View Full Email** to read the exact email the agent tried to send.

The agent rewrites a blocked email straight away using those findings. If a company's emails are blocked **three times**, the agent pauses outreach to that company for **7 days** and leaves a note asking for a human review.

## Cost by Company

The **Cost by Company** card lists the companies the agent has spent the most on, with the AI cost, tool cost and total for each.

## Controls

At the top of the agent page:

- **Start Agent**: start a paused agent. On newer accounts without Pro this shows **Upgrade to Pro to start**.
- **Pause Agent**: stop sending new outreach (the agent still handles replies).
- **Ops Room**: open the live [Ops Room](/bdr-agents/ops-room).

From the **⋯** menu:

- **Run Now (5 companies)**: process a batch straight away, inside or outside operating hours
- **Edit Configuration**: change instructions, templates or playbook, budget or other settings
- **Reset Monthly Budget**: set this month's spend back to zero
- **Delete Agent**: permanently remove the agent and all its outreach records, email threads and activity history

<Tip>
  Review escalations and draft replies regularly: these are the moments where human judgement makes the biggest difference. The agent handles the volume; you handle the nuance.
</Tip>
