The fastest way to start is to talk to Alva on [alvahq.com](https://alvahq.com). Tell her about your business, leave your work email, and she creates your account and builds a demo in it — prospects found, an outreach agent drafted, and first emails written for you to review.

## 1. Get your account

### Talk to Alva on the website (recommended)

1. Go to [alvahq.com](https://alvahq.com) and give Alva your company website.
2. Answer her questions about who you sell to, your best customers, where to look, who signs off, your plans, and why customers pick you.
3. Give her your work email address on your company's domain.

Alva then creates your account and builds your demo. When it's ready, you get an email called **Your Alva demo is ready** with a link that signs you straight in. See [Your website demo](/getting-started/website-demo) for what's in it.

### Sign up directly

1. Go to the sign-up page, enter your **work email**, and click **Continue**. You can choose **Google** instead to sign up with your Google account.
2. Enter **Your name**, **Company name**, **Company website**, a **Password** of at least eight characters, and **Confirm** it.
3. Click **Create Account**.

Alva reads your website and fills in your company profile, products, and key context, so her answers are about your business from the first conversation.

<Note>
  Alva refuses temporary or disposable email addresses. Use your work address.
</Note>

Every new account starts with a **starter credit** — no card needed. Alva is pay as you go: when the starter credit runs out, you add a card, set a monthly cap, and switch on auto top-up. See [Understanding credits](/billing/understanding-credits).

## 2. Meet Alva by email

Accounts created on alvahq.com or through the sign-up form get their own Alva email address. Alva emails you from it with a short guide to what she can do and a few questions about your business. Reply to brief her — she uses your answers to write your strategy and suggest your first searches. See [Alva by Email](/ai-assistant/alva-by-email).

## 3. Find your first prospects

Ask Alva to find companies or people that match your ideal customer, or set up a search yourself. See [Creating a Search Agent](/discovery/creating-a-discovery-profile) and [People search](/discovery/people-search).

If you already have customers or prospects in a spreadsheet, import them. See [Importing data](/crm/importing-data).

## 4. Add a company and a deal

You can also add records by hand:

- Go to **Companies** and click **New Company**. Enter the name and any details you have.
- Go to **Deals** and click **New Deal**. Link it to a company, choose a primary contact and an owner, and set the stage. Deals start at **Discovery** and move through to **Closed Won** or **Closed Lost**.

Alva scores each deal automatically as you add information. See [Managing deals](/crm/managing-deals).

## 5. Connect your email

Go to **Settings → Email Connections** and connect your Gmail or Microsoft 365 inbox. Alva reads your conversations and keeps your CRM up to date, picking out commitments, next steps, and risks without manual entry. See [Connecting an email account](/email/connecting-an-email-account).

<Tip>
  You don't need to connect your email straight away, but most of Alva's features work best once you do. A BDR agent also needs a connected mailbox before it can send.
</Tip>

## 6. Start outreach

Ask Alva to set up an outreach agent, or build one yourself. Alva previews the emails before anything is sent, and an agent only starts when you say so.

<Note>
  On new accounts, starting a BDR agent needs the Pro plan. You can build, edit, and preview agents on any plan. See [Plans and pricing](/billing/plans-and-pricing).
</Note>
