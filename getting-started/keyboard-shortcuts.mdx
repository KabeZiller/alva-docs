## Command palette

Press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows and Linux) from anywhere in Alva to open the command palette. Press it again, or **Esc**, to close it.

Before you type, the palette lists commands in groups:

| Group | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Alva** | Open Alva, or start her on a common question — your active deals, deals at risk, or pipeline health |
| **Quick Actions** | **New Company** and **New Contact** |
| **Fundraising** | Fundraising pages and investor suggestions |
| **Navigation** | Jump to **Dashboard**, **Focus**, **Companies**, **Contacts**, and more |
| **Tools** | Other areas of Alva |
| **Settings** | Open **Settings** |

Type two or more characters to search. The palette shows matching **Companies**, **Contacts**, and **Deals**, and a **Refine** group to search all companies or all contacts for what you typed, or to ask Alva about it.

Use the **arrow keys** to move through the list and **Enter** to choose.

## Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** | Open or close the command palette |
| **⌘B** / **Ctrl+B** | Collapse or expand the sidebar |
| **Esc** | Close the command palette |

## Discovery review mode

When you review a search's results one company at a time in **Review Mode**, you can work from the keyboard:

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **J** or **→** | Next company |
| **K** or **←** | Previous company |
| **I** | Import the company |
| **D** | Discard the company |
| **Esc** | Leave review mode and go back to the results |

These keys don't fire while you're typing in a text box. See [Working with your results](/discovery/working-with-your-results).

<Tip>
  The command palette is the quickest way around Alva. Instead of clicking through the sidebar, press ⌘K and type where you want to go — "focus", "Acme Corp", "settings" — and you're there.
</Tip>
