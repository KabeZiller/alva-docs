The **Setup Checklist** walks you through configuring your account step by step. It's ordered so the steps that make the biggest difference come first, and it ticks off steps automatically when it sees you've done them.

Open it from **Settings → Setup Checklist**.

## What's on the checklist

The checklist shows the steps in this order. The groups below are only a way to read the list; the app does not show them.

| Group | Steps |
|---|---|
| Teach Alva your business | **Company Profile** (with four sub-steps: **Basic Information**, **Target Market**, **Competitive Advantages**, and **Your Job Title**), **Products & Services**, **Competitors**, and **Strategy Document** |
| Bring in your existing data | **Import Your Existing Companies & Contacts** |
| Discovery | **Set Up Discovery Search Profiles (ICPs)** and **Review & Import Discovery Results** |
| Outreach | **Connect Email Accounts**, **Create an Email Signature**, **Create Email Templates**, and **Launch Your First AI BDR Agent** |
| Account, team, and CRM basics | **Choose Your Sales Methodology**, **Invite Your Team**, **Review Your Plan & Credits**, and **Get Familiar With the CRM** (optional) |

<Tip>
  If you only do one thing, make it the **Strategy Document**. Alva reads it in every conversation, so it lifts the quality of everything else. See [Strategy documents](/context/strategy-documents).
</Tip>

Each step shows:

- A title and short description
- An estimated time to complete
- Guidance on how to do it well, often with a prompt you can give Alva
- A button that takes you straight to the right page
- Whether it's done, and whether it was ticked automatically

## Steps with sub-steps

**Company Profile** is made up of four sub-steps. It counts as complete once all four are done.

## Automatic ticks

Most steps tick themselves when Alva sees the work is done — for example, when your company profile has an elevator pitch, when you've added a product or competitor, when a master strategy document exists, or when you've connected an email account. You don't need to come back and tick them by hand.

**Choose Your Sales Methodology** is the exception. Every account starts with MEDDPICC as its default, so Alva can't tell whether you've made a deliberate choice. Review your methodology in **Settings → Sales Methodologies**, then tick the step yourself. See [Sales methodologies](/crm/sales-methodologies).

## Notes

You can add your own notes to any step — for example, what you still need to do or who's handling it.

## Skipping steps

If a step doesn't apply to you, skip it. A skipped step isn't counted as complete, so it still counts towards the total when your progress percentage is worked out.

## Maturity stages

As your completion percentage rises, your account moves through six stages:

| Stage | Completion |
|---|---|
| **New Account** | 0% |
| **Getting Started** | 1% or more |
| **Configured** | 25% or more |
| **Active Account** | 50% or more |
| **Advanced Account** | 75% or more |
| **Power User** | 100% |

Only individual steps count towards the percentage — the four Company Profile sub-steps count, not the parent. To reach **Power User**, you need to complete every step, including ticking the sales methodology step and the optional CRM step.

<Tip>
  You don't have to finish the checklist to use Alva. It's a guide, not a gatekeeper — work through the steps that matter most to your business first.
</Tip>
