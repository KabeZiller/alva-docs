Your Alva account supports a whole team, each with the access they need. You manage who's on it from **Settings → Team**. Only admins can invite people or change roles.

## Roles

Every team member has one of four roles:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Full access to all settings and data, including billing and team management |
| **Manager** | Manage the team and view all data |
| **Sales Representative** | Standard CRM access for day-to-day sales work |
| **VC (Read-Only)** | Read-only access for investors. Investors don't count towards your bill |

## Inviting team members

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Click **Invite Team Member**.
3. Enter their **Email Address** and choose a **Role**.
4. Check the **Billing Impact** panel, which shows how the invite changes your monthly bill before you send it.
5. Click **Send Invitation**. They get an email with a link to join.

Invitations expire after seven days. Pending invitations are listed on the same page, where you can **Resend** or **Cancel** them.

## Email-only members

Some people, such as a founder, only want to brief Alva by email. You can add them straight away, with no invitation to accept and no login:

1. Click **Invite Team Member** and enter their email address.
2. Tick **Add straight away, email only** and choose their role.
3. Click **Add Member**.

They get a welcome email explaining how to brief Alva by emailing your account's Alva address. If they ever need the app, open the menu next to their name and choose **Send Sign-in Link** — or they can ask Alva for one. Email-only members show an **Email only** badge in the team list.

<Note>
  The email-only option appears only when [Alva by Email](/ai-assistant/alva-by-email) is switched on for your account. It's not for someone who already has a login — they get a normal invitation instead. You can also ask Alva to add an email-only member for you.
</Note>

## Managing your team

From **Settings → Team**, an admin can open the menu next to any member to:

- **Change Role**
- **Allow Strategy Edit** or **Revoke Strategy Edit** — lets a manager or sales rep edit your [strategy documents](/context/strategy-documents). Admins can always edit them. Members with this permission show a **Strategy** badge.
- **Remove from Team** — revokes their access immediately

## Running more than one company

If you work across several organisations, open the account switcher and choose **Create new company**. Enter the **Company name** and **Company website**, and Alva reads the site and sets up the new account the same way as a new sign-up. You can switch between your companies at any time from the same menu.

## Tags and custom fields

- **Settings → Tags** — create the colour-coded tags you use to categorise companies. See [Tags](/crm/tags).
- **Settings → Custom Fields** — add your own fields to company and contact records, such as the SDR who owns an account. Custom fields appear on record forms and detail pages, can be used to filter your contacts, and work as merge fields in email templates and call scripts. Admins manage the field list.

<Tip>
  Set up your tags and custom fields before you import or add lots of records. It's much easier to fill them in consistently from the start.
</Tip>
