The Focus page is your daily action list. It brings together the commitments assigned to you and the nudges Alva raises about things you might be overlooking.

Open it from **Focus** in the sidebar.

## Commitments

Commitments are things someone has said they'll do — a promise to a prospect or an action for your team. Each one is linked to a **deal**, **company**, or **contact**.

You can add a commitment:

- From the **Commitments** section on a deal, company, or contact page, using the quick add bar (type and press Enter) or the full form
- By asking Alva: "Remind me to send the proposal to Sarah by Friday"

Alva also picks up commitments from your connected email — for example, when you write "I'll send the proposal by Friday". These show an **AI detected** badge. See [Understanding email analysis](/email/understanding-email-analysis).

Each commitment has:

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Title** | What needs to be done |
| **Type** | **External (promise)** — something promised to a prospect or customer — or **Internal (action)** — a task for your team |
| **Priority** | **Urgent**, **High**, **Normal**, **Low**, or **Lowest** |
| **Due date** | Optional, but recommended |
| **Assignee** | Who's responsible. Focus shows the commitments assigned to you |

## Nudges

Nudges are suggestions from Alva — things it thinks you should look at, even though nobody committed to them. They appear in the **Signals** section of Focus, each with a short explanation and, where it helps, a suggested action. Alva raises a nudge when:

- A company or contact has gone longer without contact than the [cadence](/crm/companies-and-contacts#relationship-cadence) you set
- LinkedIn has rejected every ad in one of your campaigns — Alva offers to rewrite them
- An active LinkedIn campaign has had no impressions for three days

To snooze or dismiss a nudge, ask Alva — for example, "Snooze the Acme nudge for a week".

## How Focus is organised

Focus groups your items by urgency:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Overdue">
    Commitments past their due date, in red at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Due Today">
    Commitments due today, in amber.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Signals">
    Alva's nudges.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upcoming">
    Commitments due later, so you can plan ahead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No deadline set">
    Commitments without a due date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When there's nothing to show, Focus says **All caught up!** That's the goal.

## Completing, snoozing, and dismissing

- **Complete** — click the circle next to a commitment. It disappears from Focus straight away.
- **Snooze** — open the commitment's menu and choose **Snooze 1 day**, **Snooze 3 days**, or **Snooze 1 week**. It comes back when the snooze ends.
- **Dismiss** — open the menu and choose **Dismiss** if it no longer applies.

You can also complete commitments from the record they're attached to, or tell Alva: "Mark the Acme proposal commitment as done."

<Tip>
  Give commitments a due date. Without one, they sit in **No deadline set** at the bottom of Focus rather than rising up as they become urgent.
</Tip>

## Where commitments live

Commitments also appear on the pages of the records they're linked to. A commitment on a deal shows up both in Focus and on that deal's page, so your team sees it in context and you see it in your daily list.
