The Dashboard is the first thing you see when you sign in. It greets you by name and gives you a snapshot of your pipeline, discovery progress, and outreach in four tabs: **Overview**, **Sales**, **Discovery**, and **Outreach**.

## Overview

The Overview tab is your at-a-glance summary:

- **Headline figures** — **Pipeline Value**, **Active Deals**, **Discovery Pipeline**, and **BDR Outreach** (emails sent and reply rate)
- **Items that need your attention** — a banner that appears when something is waiting: at-risk deals, discovery results to review, priority alerts, and BDR escalations. Click any item to go straight to it
- **Pipeline chart** — your deals by stage
- **Discovery funnel** — companies searched, matched, imported, and converted
- **Discovery timeline** — how your discovery pipeline has grown over the last 30 days
- **Recent Activity** and **Top Deals**
- **AI Insights**, **Email Activity**, **Methodology**, and **Fundraising** — small cards with the latest from each

## Sales

The Sales tab goes deeper into your pipeline:

- **Key figures** — **Total Companies**, **Total Contacts**, **Active Deals**, **Win Rate**, **Closing This Month**, **At-Risk Deals**, **Average Deal Size**, and **Pipeline Value**, with the change from last month where it applies
- **Pipeline chart** — deals and value by stage
- **Methodology Adherence** — how completely your deals are filled in for MEDDPICC, BANT, or SPICED. See [Sales methodologies](/crm/sales-methodologies)
- **Top Deals** and **Recent Activity**

## Discovery

The Discovery tab shows how your prospecting is going:

- **Key figures** — **Active Profiles**, **Companies Discovered**, **High-Confidence Matches**, **Pending Review**, and **Imported to CRM**
- **Profiles by Type** — your searches split by the kind of company they look for
- **Profile Progress** — each active search with its progress and company counts
- **Search Agent cost efficiency** — the average cost per company discovered and per company enriched

See [Working with your results](/discovery/working-with-your-results).

## Outreach

The Outreach tab tracks your BDR agents:

- **Key figures** — **Active Agents**, **Companies Evaluated**, **Emails Sent**, and **Reply Rate**
- **Agent Performance** — per-agent figures and status
- **Email Intelligence** — buying and risk signals detected in your emails over the last 30 days
- **Email Activity** — outreach email engagement over the last 30 days

See [Reading the activity feed](/bdr-agents/reading-the-activity-feed) and [Outreach reporting](/reporting/outreach-reporting).

## The Ops Room

Click **Ops Room** at the top of the dashboard to open a live, read-only view of what your BDR agents, Search Agents, enrichment, and sending mailboxes are doing right now. It opens in a new tab. See [Ops Room](/bdr-agents/ops-room).

## Credit banner

If your starter credit has been used, or your account runs out of credit, a banner appears at the top of every page. Searches, email analysis, BDR agents, and Alva are paused until you add credit. The banner's **Add a card** or **Manage Billing** button takes you to the right place. See [Buying credits](/billing/buying-credits).
