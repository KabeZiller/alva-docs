Before you can make calls, your account needs at least one phone number. You buy numbers directly inside Alva — there's no separate phone system to set up — and each number works as a caller ID for outbound calls and a line that prospects can call back on.

Everything on this page lives in **Settings → Telephony**.

<Note>
  Only admins can switch calling on or off, buy or release numbers, complete regulatory verification, and change call settings. Once calling is on, everyone on the team can use the dialler with any of the account's numbers.
</Note>

## Switching on calling

An admin switches on calling once for the whole account. In **Settings → Telephony**, click **Enable Telephony**. Alva sets up everything needed behind the scenes, including a standard set of [call outcomes](/calling/call-outcomes-and-insights).

Once calling is on, the page shows **Telephony is active** and the settings below.

## Adding a number

Go to **Settings → Telephony → Phone Numbers** and click **Add Number**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a country">
    Select the country you want a number in. The list only shows countries where numbers are available to buy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a number type">
    Pick **Local**, **Mobile**, or **National**. The type list only shows the types that country has in stock. Toll-free numbers aren't offered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an optional area code">
    For a **Local** number, you can enter an area code (for example `020` for London) to get a number from a specific locality. Leave it blank to see numbers from anywhere in the country. Mobile and national numbers don't use an area code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what the number must support">
    Under **Must support**, tick **Calling**, **SMS**, or **MMS**. Only numbers that support every option you tick are shown. Tick **SMS** if you want to [text prospects](/calling/making-calls#text-messages) from this number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search and pick a number">
    Click search. Alva lists available numbers with their locality, region, what they support, and their monthly price. Choose the one you want and click **Provision**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your first number becomes the **default** — the caller ID used for outbound calls unless you pick a different one. You can add as many numbers as you need; when you make a call you choose which number to call **from**.

<Tip>
  A local number lifts answer rates — prospects are far more likely to pick up a number that looks local to them. If you call several regions or countries, add a number for each.
</Tip>

## Regulatory verification

Some countries only let you buy a number once your business has been verified under local telecoms rules. The rules are set per country **and** per number type, so a country might let you buy a local number straight away but need verification for a mobile one.

When you choose a country and type that needs verification, the **Add Number** dialog shows what's required and a **Start verification** button instead of the search results. The guided verification runs entirely inside Alva:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start verification">
    Click **Start verification**. Alva only asks for what that country and number type require — for example your business details, an address in the country, or supporting documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Your progress is saved as you go, so you can stop and come back with **Continue verification** later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and wait for approval">
    Approval isn't instant. It's reviewed after you submit, and Alva updates the status automatically when a decision comes back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Track every verification in the **Regulatory Verification** card in **Settings → Telephony**. Each one shows its country, number type, and status — **In progress**, **Pending review**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**. Use **Refresh** to check for an update, **Continue** to pick up an unfinished verification, or **Resubmit** if it was rejected (the rejection reason is shown underneath).

Once a verification is approved, you can buy numbers of that type in that country as normal.

## Managing your numbers

Each number in the **Phone Numbers** list shows:

- The full number and its type (**Local**, **Mobile**, or **National**)
- A **Default** badge on your default number
- What it supports — **Calling**, **SMS**, and **MMS**
- Its monthly price
- **Assigned to**, if the number has been assigned to a team member

From here, an admin can:

- **Forward incoming calls** — see below
- **Release** a number you no longer need, using the delete button

<Warning>
  Releasing a number is permanent. Once released, the number is gone and can't be recovered — you'd need to buy a new one, which may not be the same number.
</Warning>

## Forwarding inbound calls

When a prospect calls one of your Alva numbers back, you can have that call ring straight through to your mobile or office line.

On each number, enter the destination in **Forward incoming calls to** in full international format (for example `+447812345678`) and click **Save**. Inbound calls are then forwarded silently — the caller just hears it ringing — and your Alva number is shown as the caller ID for the best chance of connecting. Clear the field and save to switch forwarding off.

<Note>
  Forwarded calls use calling credit, so they're only forwarded while your account has enough credit. Inbound calls aren't recorded, transcribed, or added to the call log, and there's no voicemail — if the forwarded phone isn't answered, the call ends.
</Note>

## Call settings

In **Settings → Telephony → Call Settings**, an admin can set the defaults for every call made through Alva:

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Call Recording** | Records every outbound call |
| **AI Transcription** | Transcribes and analyses recorded calls |
| **No Answer Timeout (seconds)** | How long to let a call ring before treating it as unanswered in calling sessions (5–60 seconds) |

The same page is where admins write [call scripts](/calling/calling-sessions#company-context-and-script) and reset [call outcomes](/calling/call-outcomes-and-insights).

## Billing

Each number has its own monthly price, which depends on its country and type. You see the price next to each number when you search, and again in your list of numbers. Number charges are added to your subscription; releasing a number removes it from your next bill.

Calls themselves — and their recording and transcription — are paid for from your credits. You can see calling costs broken down in **Settings → Usage**. See [Understanding credits](/billing/understanding-credits).

## Switching off calling

An admin can switch off calling from **Settings → Telephony** by clicking **Disable** on the **Telephony is active** card, then **Disable telephony & release numbers**.

<Warning>
  Disabling telephony turns off calling and messaging for the whole account and **permanently releases every number** on it. Released numbers can't be reclaimed. If you switch calling back on later, you start from scratch and need to buy new numbers.
</Warning>

## Using Alva

You can ask [Alva](/ai-assistant/talking-to-alva) to handle most number tasks in chat. Alva can:

- Tell you whether calling is on and which numbers you have
- Check which countries and number types are available, and whether a country needs verification first
- Search for numbers and buy one for you — Alva always shows you the monthly price and waits for your go-ahead before buying
- Set up or clear call forwarding on a number
- Report on the status of your regulatory verifications
- Change the default recording, transcription, and no-answer timeout settings

Buying numbers and changing forwarding are admin-only, in chat as in Settings.

Alva can't switch calling on for the first time, upload verification documents, or release a number. For those, Alva points you to **Settings → Telephony**.
