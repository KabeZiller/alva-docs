A calling session turns a list of prospects into one focused dialling run. Instead of looking up each number and dialling it yourself, you build a list once and Alva walks you through it — showing the right script and company context for each call, moving you on to the next prospect, and tracking your progress as you go.

<Note>
  Calling sessions are a **Pro** feature. The dialler, click-to-dial, and call log are available on every plan. See [Plans and pricing](/billing/plans-and-pricing).
</Note>

## Starting a session

Click **Call Sessions** in the sidebar, then **New Session**.

### Build your call list

Under **Build call list from**, pick one or more sources:

- **BDR Agents** — choose one or more of your [BDR agents](/bdr-agents/overview) to load the companies in their outreach pipeline
- **Lead Sheet** — tick **All current leads** to load every company on your [Lead Sheet](/discovery/lead-sheet)
- **Tags** — choose one or more company [tags](/crm/tags)

When you pick more than one source, choose how to combine them:

| Option | What you get |
|--------|--------------|
| **Match any** | Companies that appear in at least one of your sources |
| **Match all** | Only companies that appear in every source you picked |

Click **Load contacts**. Each company becomes one or more entries in your list:

- **Direct** — a named contact at the company with a phone or mobile number
- **Main line** — the company's main switchboard number, used when none of its contacts has a number

You can also pick contacts by hand. Under **Select Contacts**, search by name, phone, or company and tick the people you want, or use **Select All** and **Clear**. Only contacts and companies with a stored phone number can go in a session.

### Session settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Session Name (optional)** | A name to find the session by later |
| **Dialling Mode** | **Manual** — you press **Dial** for each prospect. **Auto-dial** — Alva moves on and dials the next prospect automatically after you log your outcome |
| **No Answer Timeout (seconds)** | In auto-dial, how long to let an unanswered call ring before moving on (5–60 seconds) |

Click **Start Session** to open it.

## Working a session

The session screen gives you everything you need in one view.

### Your queue

On the left is your call list, with a progress bar showing how many entries you've completed. The current prospect is highlighted with a **Direct** or **Main line** badge, and you can click any pending entry to jump to it. Completed entries show a coloured dot for the outcome you logged.

### Company context and script

In the centre, Alva shows the **Company Information** for the current prospect — industry, size, location, website, and a short summary — so you're briefed before they pick up. Click **View full profile** to open the company in a new tab.

Below it sits your **Call Script**. Each script has two versions, and Alva shows the right one for each entry:

- A **Pitch** script when you're calling a named contact directly
- A **Discovery** script when you're calling a company main line

Use the dropdown to switch to a different script. Merge fields such as the contact's first name and the company name are filled in for each call. Admins write and manage scripts in **Settings → Telephony → Call Scripts**, and can mark one as the default for new sessions.

### Calling from and session stats

On the right, choose which [number](/calling/phone-numbers) you're **Calling From**, and watch your **Session Stats** update as you go:

- **Dials** — calls placed
- **Connects** — calls that connected
- **Pitches** — calls that ended in a positive outcome

The stats also show who's calling, when the session started, and how long it has run.

## Dialling and outcomes

For each prospect, click **Dial**, talk, then choose an [outcome](/calling/call-outcomes-and-insights) — exactly as with any other call. Click **Skip** to move past an entry without calling. In auto-dial mode, logging the outcome rolls you straight on to the next prospect.

You can switch between **Manual** and **Auto-dial** at the top of the screen at any point during a session.

Every call in a session is recorded, transcribed, and logged to the CRM just like any other call.

## Pausing, ending, and reopening

Use the buttons at the top of the session:

- **Pause** — take a break; click **Resume** to pick up where you left off
- **End Session** — mark the session as completed
- **Reopen Session** — on a completed session, return it to active, keeping all your completed entries so you can carry on with any that remain

The **Call Sessions** page lists all your sessions with their progress, mode, caller, created date, and status.

<Tip>
  Build your list from a BDR agent to call the exact companies your outreach is already warming up — your emails and your calls reach the same accounts, which lifts answer and conversion rates.
</Tip>

## Using Alva

You can ask [Alva](/ai-assistant/talking-to-alva) to set up and manage sessions in chat. For example: "Build me a calling session from the healthcare agent and everything tagged Priority."

Alva can:

- Build a session from the same sources as the **New Session** page — BDR agents, the Lead Sheet, and tags, combined with match any or match all — plus contacts or companies you name
- Preview how many named contacts and company main lines would be in the list before creating it
- Attach a call script and choose manual or auto-dial
- Tell you a session's progress and results, and what's left to call
- Pause, resume, or complete a session, rename it, or change its mode, timeout, or script
- Skip an entry

Alva never places calls. Once the session is ready, open it from **Call Sessions** and dial each entry yourself.
