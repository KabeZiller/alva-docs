Logging an outcome takes a second, but it's what puts the call on your CRM timelines — and every recorded call is analysed afterwards, so commitments, next steps, and buying signals land in your CRM without you writing a single note.

## Call outcomes

When a call ends, you choose an outcome that describes how it went. Each outcome has a colour, and some are marked **Positive**. Alva comes with this standard set:

| Outcome | Colour | Positive |
|---------|--------|----------|
| No Answer | Red | |
| Engaged / Busy | Red | |
| Voicemail | Amber | |
| Gatekeeper | Amber | |
| Pitched - Not Interested | Red | |
| Pitched - Interested | Green | Yes |
| Callback Requested | Blue | Yes |
| Not a Relevant Contact | Grey | |
| Wrong Number | Grey | |

The colours let you read a call list at a glance — they're the dots you see next to completed entries in a [calling session](/calling/calling-sessions). Positive outcomes count towards the **Pitches** figure in your session stats.

Each outcome you log also creates a call activity on the contact, company, and (where linked) deal timeline, so the call shows up in the full history alongside emails and meetings. A call with no outcome doesn't appear on the timelines.

<Note>
  You'll find the outcome list in **Settings → Telephony → Call Outcomes**. An admin can click **Reset to Defaults** to restore the standard set.
</Note>

### Logging an outcome later

If you closed the call without choosing an outcome, or want to add notes afterwards, ask [Alva](/ai-assistant/talking-to-alva) — for example, "Log my last call with Priya as Pitched - Interested and note she wants pricing by Friday." Alva records the outcome and notes against the call, adds it to the timelines, and completes the entry if the call was part of a calling session.

## AI call analysis

When a call is recorded and transcribed, Alva analyses the conversation in the background and pulls out structured insights from what was actually said.

### What Alva pulls out

- **Summary** — a short, plain-English recap of the call
- **Sentiment** — whether the call was positive, neutral, negative, or mixed
- **Buying signals** — moments that suggest the prospect is interested or moving forward
- **Risk signals** — anything that suggests the deal could stall or go cold
- **Objections** — concerns the prospect raised
- **Next steps** — anything that was agreed
- **Commitments** — promises made on the call, split into things you committed to the prospect and internal follow-ups for your team

### Where the insights appear

The analysis flows into the rest of Alva, so the call becomes part of your live pipeline intelligence:

- The **summary** is posted to the contact, company, and deal timelines as a call activity, so anyone on the team can catch up in seconds.
- **Commitments** become tracked action items on the related deal, company, or contact — and surface in [Focus](/getting-started/focus) so nothing slips.
- **Buying and risk signals** feed the linked deal's intelligence, alongside the signals Alva picks up from [email analysis](/email/understanding-email-analysis).
- **Objections** and other reusable lessons can be added to your [knowledge base](/email/knowledge-base), so Alva gets better at handling them over time.

You can also ask Alva about any call — "What did Tom say about their budget?" — and it answers from the transcript and analysis.

<Tip>
  Because Alva captures commitments and next steps for you, you can stay fully present on the call instead of scribbling notes — then review what was promised the moment you hang up.
</Tip>

## Billing

The AI analysis of each call uses a small amount of credit, shown as **Call Analysis** in **Settings → Usage**, alongside the voice, recording, and transcription costs of the call itself. See [Understanding credits](/billing/understanding-credits).
